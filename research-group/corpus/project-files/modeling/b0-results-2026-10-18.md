# B0 baseline fit: results

*Fit 2026-10-18 from `modeling/baseline-model-proposal-2026-10-18.md`. The full write-up, with trend and season plots, is in the repo at `docs/model/b0-baseline-2026-10-18.md` (PR #1144, merged). This baseline does not replace the served model, and nothing was published.*

## Run

- **Data.** All 1,896,504 rows of the new pipeline's 2026-10-18 build, covering every scraped area. The model trains on 1,706,854 rows, and the newest 189,650 ads are held out. Days are in UTC.
- **Compute.** A Modal L4 with 4 chains, 1,000 warmup and 1,500 draws. It took 45 minutes (19 sampling, 25 LOO and scoring) and cost about $0.81.
- **Changes from the proposal:**
  - The trend's shock-scale prior is 0.0003 a week instead of 0.001. With 0.001, only 76% of prior-predictive rents fell between $1k and $30k.
  - The furnished, short-term and unknown-bath indicators were dropped. The pipeline already excludes furnished and short-term ads, and every row has a bath count.

## Convergence: fails the gate on two trend parameters

| Parameter | R-hat | ESS |
|---|---|---|
| h (growth half-life) | 1.053 | 69 |
| τ (shock scale) | 1.021 | 158 |
| all coefficients, σ, ν, g, ν_T | ≤ 1.01 | — |

There was no refit and no seed retry. The cause is in the data: the weekly mix of ads, by area, moves the weekly mean more than sampling noise does. B0 has no location term, so the trend follows that week-to-week movement by pushing h down to about 2 days. **The trend's hyperparameters should not be read as estimates.** The coefficients converged.

- **Latest time split:** coverage is 0.801 at 80% and 0.957 at 95%, against bars of 0.79 and 0.95. The median absolute error is 20.3% and the bias is +1.1%.

## Premiums, median (95% interval)

The intervals treat rows as independent, so they are too narrow.

| Term | Premium |
|---|---|
| 1st bedroom (studio → 1) | +21.1% (20.9 to 21.2) |
| 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th bedroom | +2.7%, +2.4%, −1.0%, +4.5% |
| 2nd full bath | +62.3% (62.1 to 62.5) |
| 3rd, 4th full bath | +65.9%, +40.4% |
| Half bath | +32.0% |
| Shared space | −12.1% |
| Income restricted | −15.2% |
| Rent stabilized | −13.7% |

- **Noise.** The residual ν is 22.2. σ is 0.24 for studios, 0.28 to 0.29 for 1 and 2 bedrooms, and 0.35 for 3+.
- **Trend.** g is +2.4% a year (−0.4 to +5.1) and ν_T is 1.10. The trend bottoms on 2020-12-28 at −26.1% (−27.4 to −24.8) against early January 2020. It stands at +38.4% in early October 2026.
- **Season.** It peaks in mid-June, with a peak-to-trough range of 5.1% (3.5 to 6.6).
- **Predictive examples for mid-October 2026:**
  - 1-bed, 1-bath: median $4,280, 80% interval $2,950 to $6,230.
  - 2-bed, 2-bath: median $7,140, 80% interval $4,900 to $10,380.

**Why the bedroom and bath steps look wrong.** This is confounding, not a coding error. Median rent rises steadily with bedrooms, from $2,594 for a studio to $5,800 for 5 bedrooms. But among 1-bath ads, 2-bedroom ads ask slightly less than 1-bedroom ads ($3,100 vs $3,157), because cheaper neighbourhoods have more 2-bed, 1-bath stock. With no location term, the extra baths pick up the neighbourhood premium. B2, which adds location, is the test.

There are 3 hits and 5 misses, and one is untestable.

**The four shocks outside the COVID window:**
- **The largest, in the last week of data, is an artefact.** It comes from the data step. A page-price ask is an ad whose latest price is the one shown on its fetched listing page rather than a dated change in its price history (`pipeline/models/analysis/latest_ask.sql`, 878 "page price" and 26 "page price, digit" rows). The data step dated these 904 asks by the record's latest collection (`frontier/src/rentfrontier/b0data.py:108`). The ads were first posted between November 2012 and July 2026, but all were re-collected in mid-October 2026, so they all land in the final week, at a median of $3,250 against $4,763 for the rest of that week. The write-up's proposed fix, dating them by the price change behind the page price, doesn't work, because these asks have none. All 904 ads are off the market (564 no longer available, 245 rented, 88 draft, 7 delisted), and every record in the build was fetched on 2026-10-15 to 17, so the earliest record showing the price is no better. In 888 of them the page's price history holds only the opening price, and the page price is the ad's last ask before it left, from a cut the history doesn't list. The fix is to date these asks by the ad's off-market day (offMarketAt, or the status change for drafts), the latest day the price is known to have been asked. These ads stayed up 14 days at the median (5 to 32). 691 of the 904 share a unit with another ad in the rows.
- **The other three** are a June 2014 drop and two New Year weeks. None of them lines up with a scrape-coverage change.

## What this suggests next

1. **Fix the page-price dating** as described above, then refit B0. It is a data bug that distorts the trend's final weeks and would distort every later fit near the present.
2. **Add location (B2) before any more trend work.** Without it, the trend's hyperparameters don't converge and the bath steps carry the neighbourhood. B2's trend is the reference for which B0 shocks are real.
3. **Add size (B1)**, which is still needed to turn the bedroom steps into within-size premiums (backlog §2).

## Update 2026-10-19: page-price dates fixed (no refit)

PR #1145 (merged after review, tag archive/pr-1145) dates page-price asks by the ad's off-market day from its latest record. That is offMarketAt, or the first move off the market after the ad's last ACTIVE change when offMarketAt predates it or is missing. In-contract and review states don't count as off the market. New rows: `/data1/apartments/frontier/datasets/b0-1ed2cd98fe208d45-offmarket/rows.parquet` on thelio (sha256 7dafdc2c…2302c), 1,896,504 rows. Only the 904 page-price dates changed. All 904 found an off-market day (816 from offMarketAt, 88 drafts from status history). They now run from 2014-01-14 to 2026-10-02, and none falls in the last week. The week of Oct 12 mean ask moves from $5,055 (4,225 ads) to $5,427 (3,321 ads). B0 has not been refit on these rows, so the results above are from the old rows.
