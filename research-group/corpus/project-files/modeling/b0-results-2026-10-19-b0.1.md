# B0.1 fit: B0's model with BlackJAX ChEES-HMC (did not converge)

*Fit 2026-10-19 (run b0.1-b0-latest-97cd9e3c-offmarket, PR #1146, code `frontier/src/rentfrontier/b0_1.py`). The model is the same as B0: its NumPyro density matches B0's to 1e-9. The data is the corrected rows (b0-1ed2cd98fe208d45-offmarket). Results are on thelio under `/data1/apartments/frontier/runs/b0.1-b0-latest-97cd9e3c-offmarket`. No served-model or site change.*

## Run
- **Settings:** Modal A100-40GB, 16 chains, 300 ChEES warmup steps, 200 draws, every 5th draw kept (640 draws), diagonal mass matrix, float64.
- **Time:** 29 min wall time. The fit took 7.6 min (186 s warmup, 219 s sampling), LOO 20 min, held-out scoring 2 min.
- **Cost:** about $1.15.
- **Speed:** 0.030 s per gradient at 16 chains, 2.8× thelio's 2060.

## Did not converge: the chains never reached the posterior
| | B0 (Gibbs) | B0.1 chains |
|---|---|---|
| h (weeks) | 0.33 | chain means 31 to 80 |
| τ | — | 0.00017 to 0.0020 across chains |
| ν (residual) | 22.2 | 46 to 79, falling every round |
| ρ | 0.13 | about 0.99 |
| max R-hat / min ESS | 1.053 / 69 (h) | 15.0 / 8 (α) |

- **Warmup didn't settle.** It ended at step size 0.00056 with acceptance 0.92, against a 0.65 target, and had 72 divergences.
- **Sampling.** It had no divergences and about 33 leapfrog steps per draw.
- **Diagnosis.** Every hyperparameter trended steadily across all 10 rounds of draws, so the chains were still travelling toward the posterior, not mixing around it.
- **Likely cause.** The tiny step size points at the ridge between α and the weekly levels, with only a diagonal mass matrix.
- **No second run.** Too few draws was not the main issue, so the standing permission for a second run with more draws didn't apply.

## Scores (unconverged, shown only as a gap)
- **PSIS-LOO elpd:** −489,184 (SE 960) against B0's −357,525. That is about 132,000 worse, with 2,356 Pareto k above 0.7 against none for B0.
- **Latest split:**
  - 80% coverage 0.801 (B0 0.801);
  - 95% coverage 0.954 (B0 0.957);
  - median absolute error 21.9% (B0 20.3%);
  - bias +2.4% (B0 +1.1%).
- **Premiums:** none is a result. For example, the 1st-bedroom step is −3.7% against B0's +21.1%.

## Rerun with 1,500 warmup steps (did not converge, but the coefficients agree with B0)

*Run b0.1-b0-latest-97cd9e3c-offmarket-w1500: same code, data, card, chains and seed; 1,500 warmup steps, 500 draws, every 12th kept.*

- **Time and cost:** 42 min wall time, about $1.68. The fit took 21 min (13.2 min warmup, 6.9 min sampling), LOO 20 min. Measured time per gradient in the sampler: 0.023 s.
- **Warmup:**
  - final step size 0.00092;
  - acceptance 0.89 against a 0.65 target;
  - 72 warmup divergences, 0 during sampling;
  - 33 leapfrog steps per draw.
- **Convergence:** max R-hat 6.7 (α), min ESS 8.

**Where the 16 chains agree:**
- The bedroom, bath, flag, σ and ν terms agree with each other and with B0.
- Example chain means: 1st bedroom 0.182 to 0.189, and ν 22.1 to 23.0 (B0 22.2).
- Their R-hats are still high, because each chain's spread is tiny next to the small gaps between chains.

**Fit to the data is a tie:**
- **PSIS-LOO elpd:** −357,683 against B0's −357,525. That is 158 lower, but 11,478 Pareto k exceed 0.7, so it's unreliable.
- **Held-out elpd:** −38,618 against −38,782. That is 164 higher.
- **Held-out coverage:** the same 0.801 and 0.957.

**Reading:** the trend's split between the intercept, growth, the autocorrelated level and the slope shocks looks weakly identified. B0's Gibbs (h ≈ 0, where ESS for h was only 69) and these HMC chains (h ≈ 45 weeks, smaller τ) settle on different splits that fit about equally well. Following the project's order (data, then model structure, then sampler), the next step is a model-structure change to the trend, such as removing the overlap between α, g and the weekly levels.
