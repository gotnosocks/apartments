# quality-reads — LLM quality grades from the ads' lead photos and description text: one costed sample screen, then a full run and one fit only for an arm that clears the bar

**kind:** new data source (free counts, about $35-45 costed sample screen, gated full run and fit)

**what:** Data improvements reads; Modeling screens. (1) Free: count archived image URLs per building by row band and year (media, media_gallery_observations). Count training rows with a usable own-ad description (over 20 chars, deduped) by area x year. Attach C's effects to the 202 pilot apartments. (2) About $4-8: re-read the 680 pilot images (/data1/apartments/photo-pilot/) with a finish rubric (finish 1-5, condition 1-4, light 1-4), two readers. (3) Sample about 750 buildings: 600 with 21+ training rows, stratified by PLUTO class family x area, for precise targets; 150 with 1-20 rows to check transfer. Take two ads per building from different units and years. Photo arm: 3 lead photos per ad (about 3,600 images) on the finish rubric. Text arm: the same ads' descriptions, topped up to 2,000 ads, rated on UAD condition C1-C6 and quality Q1-Q6 from physical facts only, with evidence spans, dollar, fee and concession text masked, and 200 hand labels. Both arms get a 20% second read. (4) Free screen on thelio, each arm its own family: building means against C's static building part (explain-screen-v2 target, lot folds, 200 permutations; the current level screen can stand in); per-ad grades against repeat units' effects and once-listed residuals (within-building R^2); the offset change in the sample's new-building miss. (5) Only an arm that clears the gate goes to Ben for a second costed OK: a full run with as-of building means (strictly earlier ads, as elevfill does), then one Modal fit of C on nb8-nostuy-photoq-v1 or nb8-nostuy-textq-v1, plus the new-building score. If both arms pass, the one with the larger offset is fitted first; the other is a separate later fit.

**weakness:** Building terms are the largest descriptive block: building 10.2 + building over time 5.6 + slopes 4.1 of C's 26.1%. No fit has moved the new-building miss (11.9-12.5%; 17.3% in 1-5-row buildings). Screen passers explain 1.4-2.4% of building variance, against the ~9% a 0.5 pp cut needs. Quality (finish, renovation, condition, light) is the textbook omitted variable and is unmeasured. Once-listed units (47% of units) are 1.35-1.5x wider, and nothing known at listing identifies them.

**mechanism:** Renters pay for visible finish, condition and light. These differ between buildings of the same era, class and amenities, and between units in one building. A grade read from a new building's first ads enters the fixed part, the only part that reaches an unseen building. It moves variance from the building level, walk and unit effect into a named feature. Text grades vary within a building and over a unit's life (renovation between listings), so PSIS-LOO can credit them. One ordinal pair pools the many weak cues that about 30 regex flags lost in noise.

**evidence:**
- Descriptive share 26.1% (25.4-26.8): building 10.2, over time 5.6, slopes 4.1, unit 2.8 (newbuilding-2026-10-13.md:54-61). New-building miss 11.9%; 1-5 rows 17.3%; 6-20 rows 14.3% (:23-27).
- A 0.5 pp cut needs ~9% of building variance; screen passers explain 1.4-2.4% (reader finding 8; prior-review-rent-model-review:249).
- The only explanatory wins are within-building ad facts: coded +109.4 +- 30.7, bedtext +73.1 +- 24.6, relist +39.9 +- 14.9 (feature-tests.md:48-51). The regex versions were weak: attrs-v1 -8.0 +- 21.8 (:27), renov +2.0 +- 14.8 (:75), text-v1 +43.7 +- 23.5 (:73).
- Photo pilot: 202 apartments, 680 images, about 1.3M tokens, under $4 (about $0.006/image). Windows agreed 68%. A new costed OK is needed, and no new LLM calls or image downloads may run without approval (data-improvements.md:763-768, :815).
- Image URLs and media JSON are archived without binaries (granular-dataset.md:212-218). Descriptions exist on 10,381 of 27,341 NB5-era in-fit singles (research-backlog.md:1059).
- Largest Chelsea building offsets were quality and access facts: luxury finishes +50 to +60%, rent-stabilization wording -35%, basements -28%, shared bathrooms -27% (chelsea-group-effects-2026-09-18.md:11-16).
- Prior review: what is left in building levels is finishes, renovation, light and management; screen a sample before any fit and fit only at 5% or more (prior-review-rent-model-review:318-319, 327, 347). Stopping rule (brief): move effort to size and quality data.
- Power (inferred): with 600 buildings, the SE of an R^2 near 0.09 is about 0.022.

**prediction:**
- psis_loo: Screen: n/a. Gated photo (building-level) fit: +50 (80% -20 to +160), clearing 2 clustered SE with P about 0.45. Gated text (per-ad) fit: +150 (80% -20 to +500), clearing 2 clustered SE with P about 0.5.
- new_building: Gated fit: miss 11.9 -> 11.6 (-0.3 pp, 80% -0.6 to -0.05); 1-5-row bin -0.8 pp (-2.0 to +0.2); 80/95 coverage within +-0.4. Unconditional (the screen will usually stop it): about -0.1 pp.
- new_unit: Photo building means: n/a (+-1 pp on the 964 rows). Per-ad grades, if fitted: 80% coverage +1 pp (80% -0.5 to +3), from the once-listed tails.
- descriptive_share: Gated fit: -0.6 pp (80% -1.2 to -0.1), at the 0.6-0.7 pp noise floor.
- other: Pilot re-read: weighted kappa 0.65 (0.5-0.8); correlation with C's building+unit effect r = 0.25 (0.1-0.4). Photo screen: 4% of building variance (80% 1.5-9%; null_95 about 0.5-0.8%); 6% (2-13%) in 1-20-row buildings; P(5% or more overall) about 0.38; offset -0.2 pp (0 to -0.5). Text screen: within-building R^2 3.5% (2-6%); building share 1.5% (0-6%). Fit coefficients: +3 to +6% per finish step; Q5-6 vs Q3-4 +6 to +15%; the desc-v1 renovated and luxury coefficients shrink by 50% or more.

**cost:** Modal $0 for the screen; about $2.1 per gated fit. Oxylabs $0 if images fetch directly as in the pilot (unverified). If they must go through Oxylabs: up to $4 for the sample and $18-24 for a full photo run. LLM: $4-8 pilot re-read, about $22-25 photo sample, $5-10 text sample, about $4 second reads (about $35-45 to the screen verdict). Full runs: about $100-130 for photos and $150-600 for text at the pilot's rate (3-5x less with a cheaper model or batch; $0 on a local model on thelio); each needs a quote. Effort: Data improvements 2-3 days; Modeling about 0.5 day; about 3-4 days to the screen verdict. Approvals: Ben's costed OK for the sample and the image fetch path, then for any full run.

**free_precheck:** Free counts: kill the photo arm if fewer than 70% of 1-20-row buildings have 2 or more photos, or fewer than 50% of 2014+ ads have an image. Kill the text arm if fewer than 60% of training rows have a usable description. Pilot re-read (about $6): kill the photo arm if weighted kappa < 0.5 or |r| with C's effects < 0.1; kill the text arm if kappa < 0.6. Screen gate for a photo full run: building share at least 5% and above null_95 overall, or at least 8% in 1-20-row buildings, or an offset of -0.2 pp or better. The text arm stops if within-building R^2 < 1% and building share <= max(2%, null_95).

**miss_criteria:** Photo screen miss: under 5% overall, under 8% in 1-20-row buildings, and an offset weaker than -0.2 pp; recorded as a miss of the claim that building levels are mostly finishes. Text screen miss: the stop condition above, or kappa < 0.6. Fit miss: the new-building miss falls by less than 0.15 pp (photo) or 0.1 pp (text) while the descriptive share falls by less than 0.3 pp and the PSIS-LOO gain is under 2 clustered SE; or PSIS-LOO falls below -2 SE.

**p_success:** 0.35

**expected_to_fail_reason:** Each arm is likelier to fail than to pass (photos P about 0.27-0.3, text about 0.35, and the two are correlated). C already carries finish proxies: era, class, alteration, doorman, and the renovated, luxury and stainless flags. Regex text versions tied. Lead photos are staged marketing. Pilot window answers agreed only 68%. Building levels may be micro-location, landlord pricing and lease-up timing. Broker prose can encode the price tier, a prevprice-type validity risk the physical-facts rule must police.

**worth_it_anyway:** For about $35-45 it settles the quality hypothesis on which both prior reviews and the stopping rule rest. A clean null says to stop buying building-level explanation data for these 8 areas and to spend on contrast areas instead. A pass gives the first non-location explanation of building levels. Per-ad grades may explain part of the once-listed spread, and the evidence spans flag data errors (basement units, shared baths).

**rank_argument:** It is the only lever any lens found with a plausible path to the ~9% of building variance a 0.5 pp new-building cut needs: location is saturated, size is capped near 0.2 pp, and existing families land at 1-2.4%. It puts explanation in the fixed part and is cheap to falsify, with hard gates before the larger spend. It ranks below explain-screen-v2 only because v2's ceiling first says whether existing data could get there, and v2 is the right instrument to score the sample.

**depends_on:** Ben's costed OKs: the sample and fetch path first, then any full run. explain-screen-v2's target (soft; the level screen can stand in). nb-lpd-clustered SEs to judge a fit. mw-external rows as an external yardstick for a passing grade.
