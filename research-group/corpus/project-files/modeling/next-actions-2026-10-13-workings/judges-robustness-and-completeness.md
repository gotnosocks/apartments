# COMPLETENESS CRITIC

## uncovered weaknesses
- New-unit coverage is too narrow: 67/89 on 964 by-product rows, and 0.62/0.87 for units that never relist (research-backlog.md:1094-1107). It is the one proven range defect, and nothing in the canon fixes it. time-split only measures it. The deferrals are circular: the uncertainty lens holds 2i until time-split exists (judge-pack:1244), and both time-split critics say to defer time-split until 2i is queued (judge-pack:459, :485). Line (stack) effects were rejected as 'memory'; their config exists only for the design before sizeunkslope (model.py:1845-1858). The unknown-ness multipliers that the prior review marked 'Run' were never carried forward.
- Small buildings: the 1-5-row bin misses by 17.3% with 77.8/92.3 coverage, and the 6-20-row bin by 14.3%. The only candidate aimed at them is walk-ar1's bscale branch. Its cited support (c = 1.33-1.37) is a per-cell Student-t noise multiplier on the served predictive, i.e. row noise, not building dispersion (verified, research-backlog.md:254-266). No explanatory candidate has the power to show a gain there: quality-reads' 150-building arm has an R^2 SE of about 4 pp. The board's headline is row-weighted, and the 1-5 bin is only 2.1% of rows, so even a real gain there is invisible.
- Neighbourhood labels as a crutch: labels are about 3.4 pp of the 26.1% descriptive share (inferred), and the West Village premium of about 9 pp is unexplained. No candidate removes or explains the labels. mw-external only measures them, and its in-area rung is arithmetic on C's label posterior. wv-nta-step adds another label term. The label-free fit was rejected (see dropped_too_early).
- Per-building slopes hold 4.1 pp of the descriptive share, and C moved variance from the building level into them. No candidate explains them with building facts. explain-screen-v2 adds a slope target but proposes no family to score on it.
