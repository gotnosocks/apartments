# B0.2 fit: results (did not converge)

*Fit 2026-10-19. B0.2 is B0.1 with the trend replaced by a random walk with drift and Student-t shocks (non-centered), and the bedroom and full-bath steps replaced by a 2-D cumulative ladder anchored at a studio with 1 bath (PR #1148, merged as 979dc59f). Same off-market rows as B0.1. Nothing on the served model or the site changed.*

## Run

- Run `b0.2-b0-latest-979dc59f-b0.2-offmarket-w1500` in `/data1/apartments/frontier/runs/` on thelio.
- Modal A100-40GB, 16 chains, 1,500 warmup, 500 draws (keeping every 12th), at most 64 leapfrog steps.
- 43 minutes in all (fit 22 min, LOO 20 min). It cost about $1.70 against an estimate of $1.68.

## Convergence: fails the gate

| Parameter | R-hat | ESS |
|---|---|---|
| g (drift) | 5.8 | 8 |
| τ (shock scale) | 2.35 | 14 |
| s_int (interaction scale) | 1.99 | 25 |
| ν_T (shock tail) | 1.08 | 194 |
| Ladder, worst of 23 steps | 5.9 (full≥3) | 9 |
| ν and the 4 σ's | ≤ 1.005 | > 1,200 |

There were 751 divergences in warmup and none in sampling, and the step size settled at 0.0014. The figures below are unconverged and are not results.

## Why

1. **Data: the trend grid starts too early.** The grid starts on 2010-07-26 because 18 rows are dated before December 2013. Dense data begins in December 2013 (2,054 rows that month and at least 665 a week from 2014). So about 177 weeks of walk have almost nothing under them.
   - The chains disagree on the early level: α ranges from 7.73 to 8.05 across chains.
   - That drags the drift with it: chain medians for g run from −1.2% to +2.2% a year.
   - From 2014 on, α + L agrees across chains to within 0.01.
2. **Structure: τ and the shocks trade off.** τ settles near 0.037, but the weekly shocks the fit actually uses have sd 0.0064, so the standardized shocks sit at about a sixth of their prior scale. With 1.7M rows fixing each week's level, a non-centered walk mixes slowly along that trade-off. A centered walk is the usual fix when the data pin the states this tightly.
3. **s_int = 0.65 against a HalfNormal(0.1) prior.** The bath steps differ a lot by bedroom level, which supports re-anchoring them (B0.3).

## Scorecard against the predictions in the PR

- **Hits (6 of 8 cell and trend predictions):**
  - 1 bed over studio, +21.4%;
  - 2 bed over 1 bed, +1.1%;
  - 2nd bath at 2 beds, +71.3%;
  - the 2nd bath is worth more at 2 beds than at 1;
  - 2020 trough, −23.6%;
  - season peak in June.
- **Misses:**
  - s_int was 0.65, against a predicted 0.05 to 0.30;
  - trend: g was +0.6%/yr, τ 0.037 and ν_T 15.8, against +1 to +4%, 0.0012 to 0.004 and under 2. With ν_T near 16 the shocks came out close to Gaussian, but the trend didn't converge, so this isn't a finding.
- **Convergence:** missed.
- **Wall time under 1 hour:** hit.
- **Held-out coverage:** 0.803 and 0.956, a hit.
- **LOO:** −350,212 (SE 1,009) against B0.1's −357,683. The paired difference on shared rows is +7,470 (SE 158). No Pareto k exceeds 0.7, where B0.1 had 11,478. Neither model converged, so this is not a win under the framework.
