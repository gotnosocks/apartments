# Bedroom-count review: sample listings (2026-10-15)

The screen flags rows whose coded bedroom count is far below what their price suggests for that building (for example, a "studio" priced like the building's 3BRs). All 1,570 flagged NB9 rows have been read: the ad itself, plus the same unit's other ads.

Results so far:
- **Corrected from the ad text:** 83 rows
- **Studio confirmed by the ad:** 558
- **No usable count:** 625, mostly blank ads, ads that just say "apt", and The Archive's duplex lofts
- **Positive count with no number in the ad, kept as coded:** 299
- **Bedroom described without a number:** 4

Links go to `https://streeteasy.com/rental/<listing>`.
