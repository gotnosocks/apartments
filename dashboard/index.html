Accuracy is PSIS-LOO ΔELPD: each training listing's log-rent density given all the others, summed over the row split's 47,374 training rows and paired against the m0-base baseline. Fit time is the scored fit's sampler wall time on the hardware it used. Thelio fits run one at a time; from commit 3c26c4a each records how many cores other processes used meanwhile (Timing: clean below 0.5). See the research plan.

Pareto frontier: PSIS-LOO ΔELPD × fit time

For the selected hardware: up is more accurate, left is faster. Each selected hardware class has its own staircase, its frontier: entries on that hardware that no other gate-passing entry on the same hardware beats on both axes at once (a fit time only competes with fit times on the same hardware). Colour is the sampler and shape is the hardware. Whiskers are ±1 combined SE (paired SE and Monte Carlo error). The gray staircase is the latest frontier, for context when scrubbing back in time. PyMC screens saved no posterior draws, so they have no PSIS-LOO score and appear only in the table.

Same model, different implementations

Each row is one model structure fit by more than one implementation (custom Gibbs, PyMC, NumPyro) or on more than one kind of hardware, across all hardware classes, not just the one selected above. Implementations that agree on PSIS-LOO confirm each other's convergence; the right-hand column is the spread of PSIS-LOO across the passing fits. The fastest passing mark is the structure's best implementation on that hardware. Latest fit per implementation and hardware.

Where the variation goes

For each frontier entry on this hardware: the share of the variation in log rent over the training rows that the fit attributes to each part of the model, and what is left over (covariance attribution; shares sum to 100%). A gain in accuracy that comes with a larger unit share explains less: it credits the apartment's identity, not named features.

Validation: PSIS-LOO vs held-out rows

Each entry's PSIS-LOO score against its genuine held-out score (5,264 rows its fit never saw, vs the promoted model). If PSIS-LOO measures the same thing, points rise from left to right.

Unit split (secondary)

All listings of about 10% of units held out: prediction for units the fit never saw. Secondary evidence; not part of the ranking or the frontier.
