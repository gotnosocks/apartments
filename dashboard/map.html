About this map

One dot per building on a map of Chelsea turned to Manhattan's street grid (avenues run up the page, the Hudson is to the left). Streets are drawn at their recorded width; parks and the shoreline are from NYC Open Data. Colour is how the building's typical asking rent (posterior median) compares with Chelsea's median building that year, for the chosen bedroom count: blue cheaper, red dearer, gray within 5%. The chart below shows how the level itself moved.

Buildings are shown in the years from their first to their last listing in the fit; outside those years their rent is the model's extrapolation (tick the box above to show them, hollow). Hover for the rent in dollars; zoom with the buttons or a double click, and drag to pan.
