What these measures mean

PSIS-LOO ΔELPD
How well a fit predicts each listing's asking rent from all the other listings, compared with the simplest baseline model. It is summed over the {{ "{:,}".format(meta.provenance.rows_in_fit) if meta.provenance.rows_in_fit else "" }} listings in the fit, on a log scale; higher is better. Two fits within about two standard errors (the ±) are a tie.
Fit time
Minutes from start to finish to fit the model to the full dataset on one machine's hardware. Only fits on the same hardware compare.
Complexity
How much a design asks a renter to understand, judged by the researcher and written down in rentfrontier.elegance. Each structural term (the market over time, a building's premium, a building's path over time, per-building slopes, unusual apartments) and each layer of facts (what the listing offers, what the ad says, the city's building facts, which streets the windows face, the neighbourhood) adds points. Terms that move over time or overlap another term cost more. Lower is simpler.
Convergence checks
Whether the sampler settled on one stable answer (R-hat and effective sample size). A fit that fails them is shown hollow and never served.
Subset fits
Fits on a part of the data, used to tune settings quickly. They are exploration only, never served.
