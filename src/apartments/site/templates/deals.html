Deals

The apartments on the market{% if info.scoring_day %} on {{ day(info.scoring_day) }}{% endif %}, ordered by how low each ask is for that apartment. For every ad the model gives a range of asks it would expect, from what it knows about the apartment, its building, its block and the date, and from the apartment's own earlier ads when it has any. Lower than 95% means the ask is below 95% of the asks the model would expect for this apartment.

A low ask is a lead, not a verdict. The model describes asking rents and cannot see an apartment's condition, a broker fee, a short lease or a mistake in the ad. The range is tighter for an apartment with recent ads of its own, so the same gap counts for more there. An ask with free months is the rent net of them, so the ad's own page shows a higher rent. Free months are part of what the model knows about an ad: the range is for an ad with the same concession. Some low asks have a plain reason the model does not know: the ad says the apartment is income-restricted, it reads like an office, the apartment has been listed with another bedroom count, or the ad is off the market for now. Those ads carry a caution and are left out of the list unless you include them.
