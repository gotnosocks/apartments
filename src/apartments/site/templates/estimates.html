What the model expected each ad to ask

For each ad here, the served model gives the range of asks it would expect for that apartment, on that date, from what it knows about the apartment, its building, its block and the market, and from the apartment's own earlier ads when it has any. It never saw the ask it is compared with. Each past ad is the newest ad of its apartment, predicted by a fit that learned from every earlier ad but not that one. Each ad on the market is scored by a fit made without the asks on the market{% if info.scoring_day %} on {{ day(info.scoring_day) }}{% endif %}. So the gap between an ad's ask and the model's middle estimate is a fair reading of whether it asks more or less than the model expects.
