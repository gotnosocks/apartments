Simplicity

Whether a model is easy for a renter to understand as a whole. For pairs of models that matter (tied on accuracy, or where one beats the other on accuracy and speed), two independent AI judges read both model definitions without seeing any scores. Each says which one a renter would find simpler, or that they are about equal, and why. If they disagree, the pair counts as equal.

A design is a model with its features; the data rules and the sampler do not change it. When two fits predict equally well, the simpler design is served, and a fit judged simpler than one that beats it on accuracy and fit time stays on the frontier. Two designs no judge has compared count as equally simple.
