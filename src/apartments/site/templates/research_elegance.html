Elegance

Elegance is how coherent a model is as a statistical model of how asks arise. A more elegant model tells one generative story whose structure mirrors the data (apartments in buildings in neighbourhoods, over time). It uses few distinct mechanisms, applied consistently, and has no overlapping or competing terms. It avoids one-off flags, hand-tuned cutoffs and patches, uses natural forms for its likelihood and priors, and needs no more structural choices than what it captures. For pairs of models that matter, two independent AI judges read both model definitions without seeing any scores. Each says which one is more elegant, or that they are about equal, and why. If they disagree, the pair counts as equal. Ties in accuracy go to the more elegant model.

A design is a model with its features; the data rules and the sampler do not change it. A fit judged more elegant than one that beats it on accuracy and fit time stays on the frontier. Two designs no judge has compared count as equally elegant. Beside each design is its effective number of parameters, an objective check measured from the data.
