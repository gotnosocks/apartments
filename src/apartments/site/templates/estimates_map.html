About this map

One dot per building on a map turned to Manhattan's street grid (avenues run up the page, the Hudson is to the left). Colour is how the building's typical asking rent compares with the median building that year, for the chosen bedroom count: blue cheaper, red dearer, gray within 5%. The chart below shows how the level itself moved.

The median building's rent is what a typical apartment of that size asks in the middle building of the area, features included, so it is higher than the Estimates page's market reference, a stripped-down reference apartment. Intervals here are 90%, where most other pages show 95%: the map follows the rent-map summary, which reports 90% intervals.

Buildings are shown in the years from their first to their last listing in the fit; outside those years their rent is the model's extrapolation (tick the box above to show them, hollow). Hover for the rent in dollars; zoom with the buttons or a double click, and drag to pan.
