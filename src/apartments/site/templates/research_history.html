Predictions made before fitting (all hardware)

Before a test fit runs, the thread that queues it writes down what it expects: how much the fit should gain or lose against a named fit, as a range of ΔELPD on the same listings, and whether the gain should clear the bar of about twice its margin for luck. Once the fit lands, the prediction is scored. A hit means the result fell in the range and cleared, or stayed within, the bar as predicted. Writing predictions first keeps the research honest about what it expected, rather than explaining results afterwards. {{ predictions | length }} recorded{% if scored %}: {{ hits | length }} hit{{ "" if hits | length == 1 else "s" }} and {{ scored | length - hits | length }} miss{{ "" if scored | length - hits | length == 1 else "es" }} so far{% endif %}{% if predictions | length > scored | length %}, {{ predictions | length - scored | length }} waiting for a fit or not scored on this split{% endif %}.
