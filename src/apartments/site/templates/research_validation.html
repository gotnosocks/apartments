Validation

Checks that the accuracy measure can be trusted: whether the score each fit estimates for itself agrees with a genuine held-out test, what each model explains, and whether the same model gives the same answer in different code.

The estimated score against a real hold-out

The board ranks fits by PSIS-LOO, a shortcut that estimates from one fit how well it would predict each listing it had not seen. Each fit also kept some listings out entirely; their held-out score is the real thing.
