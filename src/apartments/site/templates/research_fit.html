Each split

The row split holds out some listings of repeat-listed units; the unit split holds out whole apartments. ΔELPD here is on the held-out listings, against the reference model. The diagnostics check that the sampler settled on one stable answer: R-hat compares independent runs (below 1.01 means they agree), ESS is how many independent draws the answer rests on (above 400 is enough), the worst group R-hat is the same check over every building and unit effect (below 1.05), and divergences count places the sampler could not explore (none is right).
