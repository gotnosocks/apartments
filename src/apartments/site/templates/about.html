How the estimates work

What an estimate is

A listing's estimate is the model's typical asking rent for that apartment, in that building, in that month: the median of the asks it expects, in dollars a month. It combines four things, each learned from all {{ "{:,}".format(s.listings) }} listings:

  • the market that month (a trend and a calendar season{% if "bedroom_market_curve" in names %}, and each bedroom group's own path{% endif %});
  • the apartment's features: bedrooms, baths, size, floor, building amenities, views, and flags from the ad text;
  • the building: {{ building[:-1] | join(", ") }}{% if building | length > 1 %} and {% endif %}{{ building[-1] }};
  • the unit itself, learned from its other listings.

Each estimate leaves out the listing's own ask (leave-own-row-out). A model that had seen the ask would be pulled toward it, and every listing would look fairly priced. Leaving the ask out makes the gap between ask and estimate an honest signal. The page shows the in-sample fit separately.

Below typical and Above typical mean that the ask is in the lowest or highest {{ (100 * meta.price_bands[0]) | round | int }}% of the asks the model expects for that apartment, noise included. The 95% range is the uncertainty of the typical ask itself, not the spread of individual asks.

Estimates describe asking rents on StreetEasy, not leases, net effective rents or appraisals. Listings from before {{ month(s.last_period) }} show the first ask of each advertisement. The model cannot see anything missing from the listing data, such as renovations the ad does not mention, light or noise.

Listings left out

Some listings are not an open-market lease of a whole apartment at their address: offices and shops, ads that place the apartment elsewhere, single rooms, income-restricted and short-stay offers. A review of their own ads found them, and the model leaves them out, so they get no estimate. They are listed with their reasons under Left out.

The model's design, how it was chosen, its convergence checks and every feature's effect are on the research side: the served model.
