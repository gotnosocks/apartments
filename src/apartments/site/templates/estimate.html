Percentages at the posterior mean, each against the reference level of its inputs; multiplied together they give roughly the typical rent, which also averages over how this apartment might differ from the building's usual. "This building" is its own level, trend and bedroom and size premiums. A feature's line is only what it adds on its own: features that come with pricier apartments, such as a penthouse or a view, can look small because much of their premium sits in each apartment's own level, which the model learns from its listings and cannot know for an apartment it has not seen.
