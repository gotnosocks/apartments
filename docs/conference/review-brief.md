# Referee brief for the outside-model panel

You are a referee for a short paper submitted to a conference on housing and urban economics with an applied-statistics audience. The paper (paper-r2.txt in this folder; figures are described by their captions) is revision 2, already revised once after three reviews (a statistician, a housing economist and a skeptical referee); the changes are listed at its end.

Review it on the statistics (model, priors, identification, sampler and convergence, validation design, uncertainty claims), the economics (hedonic interpretation, sample selection, asking vs contract rents, regulation, literature), and the claims most likely to fail a hostile question at the talk (overclaiming, forking paths, internal inconsistencies, numbers that don't add up).

Return at most 500 words: a recommendation (accept / minor revision / major revision / reject) with one sentence why; the 3 to 6 most important weaknesses, each with a concrete fix; any internal inconsistency you found; and one or two strengths. Be direct and specific. Cite only literature you are confident exists.
