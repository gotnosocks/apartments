# Blog discussion brief

The post in blog.txt has just gone up on a data blog about New York housing. Its readers are a demanding enthusiast community: quants, urbanists, economists, data-viz people, renters and brokers who know the city. They read closely, check numbers, and push hard on claims; the bar is as high as a referee's, but the register is an open-internet comment thread, not a formal review. Think of the best comment sections and response posts you've seen: specific, argumentative, sometimes funny, always about the substance.

## Round 1: your top-level comment

Write one top-level comment (at most 350 words) under the handle you are given. Engage the research problem itself, not just the write-up: what you think is actually going on in the data, what the post gets right, what it gets wrong or oversells, what you would test next, and any NYC knowledge that bears on it. Be concrete: name numbers from the post, propose a specific check, or offer a competing explanation. Plain text or light Markdown. No sign-off, no headings, no bullet-point referee template unless it really helps. Cite only work you are confident exists.

## Round 2: your reply

You will then see every round-1 comment. Write one reply (at most 200 words) to the comment you most want to push back on or build on, starting with "@handle". Disagree where you disagree. You may also answer a point raised about your own comment.
