# Research record: the pricing model

This file lists what the project has tried on the pricing model, what each try reported, and what became of it. It is built from the docs in the repo, mainly `docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/model/`, `docs/notes/` and `docs/handoff/`. Numbers are copied as the sources give them. "Not stated" means the source read does not give the number. It covers work through 2026-10-24, the date of the last dated model note. The drift model (2026-11-10) is written up separately.

How to read the scores:

- "PSIS-LOO" is the paired change in expected log density (elpd) on rows both fits keep. The unit is nats. "± x" is the standard error as the source gives it. A gain needs about 2 SE.
- "Held-out" is the score on rows the fit never saw. Its meaning changed over time. Early on it was a 10% row split from repeat-listed units. From October it is mostly the "latest split" (see `docs/leak-free-scoring.md`), and later a whole-building split.
- "Gate" is the convergence gate: split R-hat at most 1.01 and bulk ESS above 400, with extra checks on group effects and later on every building effect (`docs/model/leaderboard/leaderboard.md`, `docs/handoff/modeling.md`).
- "Served" means the fit became the model behind the site.

## 1. The arc

The first models were small Bayesian rent indexes for one building, then three buildings (August 2026), then a pooled all-Chelsea model (2026-09-08). A canonical-unit reset in mid September produced a fast robust point model and a separate amenity research line. From 2026-09-18 the main line was a PyMC log-rent model with explicit baths, source-reviewed current rows, a natural cubic spline for listed floor, bedroom-group time curves and a per-building half-year walk (2026-09-22 and 09-23). On 2026-09-24 the work moved to a "frontier" line: a custom Gibbs sampler on GPU, scored by PSIS-LOO against fit time, with designs `m0` to `m8` and then a long run of data rules, features and noise terms. That line added West Village and other neighborhoods (NB5, NB8, NB9), then all scraped Manhattan and Brooklyn listings. The all-scraped Gibbs fit was served on 2026-10-17 by Ben's hand, over a failed convergence gate (`config/main-analysis.json`).

From 2026-10-18 a new line, "B0", started over on 1.9 million ads with a simple mean (bedrooms, baths, weekly trend, season, flags) and added pieces one at a time: a normal-shock walk, a map per zone, then a level for every building around its zone's map. This is the "building zones" line. Its fits were short Modal runs, each with predictions written first and a results note after. The line settled on a base model, `building_zones_floor` (floor curves by elevator or walk-up), then an as-of version that builds every aggregate from the row's own ad and earlier training ads only. "Fit 8" raised the elevator floor curve to 64. "Fit 4" added a random effect per unit and unit means. It improved the latest-split score most but misses the convergence gate. The "kitchen sink" added every unit and building column that could be coded, without map or distance columns. Its 1 km fit passes the gate and beats fit 8. The 150 m twin ran out of GPU memory. Nothing in the building-zones line is served.

As of 2026-11-09, the docs hold no dated model result after 2026-10-24. The kitchen-sink thread stopped then: no fits queued, fit (b) blocked by GPU memory, fit (c) cancelled (`docs/handoff/kitchen-sink.md`). The conference follow-up thread was paused on 2026-10-23 with fit 5 not launched (`docs/handoff/conference-followups.md`). The model behind the site is still the all-scraped Gibbs fit that failed the gate.

On 2026-11-10 Ben changed the aim to a descriptive model that finds deals with honest uncertainty, and one thread rebuilt the model in a day (part F of the ledger). Two residual checks showed that an apartment's level fades over years and that a building's units move together for a few months. The `drift` model adds a unit level that lasts plus one that drifts, and after six rounds on the sampler its three full fits passed the gate: held-out elpd 195,320 against the kitchen sink's 156,105. It is the served model, and the site's Deals page is published from it. `drift-building` adds a quarterly path per building and scores 230,885; its fits miss the R-hat line by 0.0006 to 0.0035 on one quantity each and it is not served.

## 3. What worked

- **Unit-level data fixes.** Unit-consistent bedrooms (+791 ± 73), unit square feet (+653 ± 53) and unit label flags (+234 ± 29) together gave +1,679 ± 95 on m0q. `docs/research-plan.md`
- **A per-building time term.** The half-year walk gave +825.5 ± 45.0 held-out on 2026-09-23. A linear building trend gave +3,165 ± 93 on the frontier. `docs/model/building-drift-experiment-2026-09-23.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`
- **Heavy-tailed unit levels and noise by bedroom group.** Student-t units gave +2,009.2 ± 89.4. A residual scale per bedroom group gave about +360 to +500 across tests. A scale by bedroom group and year gave +745.3 ± 53.8 and +812.4 ± 55.1. `docs/model/feature-tests.md`
- **A custom Gibbs sampler on GPU.** From 702 s on, Gibbs fits beat NUTS fits of the same designs on score and time. `docs/research-plan.md`
- **Rent-blind data rules.** Unit labels, registry corrections and quarantine rules were mostly ties on score. The value was correct served facts and cleaner rows. `docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/handoff/modeling.md`
- **Fixing data before the model.** The $1,000 row at 15 E 17th St #4 and the west-coast lot with two structures explained gate failures that more sampling could not fix. `docs/model/arwalk-2026-10-14.md`, `docs/handoff/modeling.md`
- **A spline for floor.** The spline cut the floor design from 93 to 47 columns and the leapfrog work from 6,959,168 to 1,512,000 transitions. `docs/model/main-model-evolution.md`
- **A map plus a level per building, on Gibbs.** The Gibbs building-zones sampler ran in about 9 to 25 minutes per fit at under $1.60 each. `docs/notes/building-zones-gibbs-2026-10-20.md`
- **Floor curves by elevator.** +2,945 (SE 83) held-out elpd over the age run, about 17 two-way SEs. `docs/notes/conference-followups-2026-10-22.md`
- **Raising the elevator curve cap to 64.** +52 elpd (SE 24) overall, and the 105 ads at floor 47 moved by 4 SE. `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof3-cap64-2026-10-23.md`
- **Unit effects for repeat listings.** Fit 4 gained +28,903 held-out elpd on the latest split, where every held-out ad has a unit with training rows. `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof3-unitmean-2026-10-23.md`
- **Many extra columns.** The kitchen sink added +7,871 elpd over fit 8 and kept interval coverage. `docs/notes/kitchen-sink-2026-10-23.md`
- **Park-facing view premium within buildings.** +6.7% by the ad's view mark and +10.5% by the line's. `docs/notes/park-placebo-2026-10-23.md`
- **Writing predictions before fits.** Every building-zones note scores its predictions as hits and misses, for example fit 8 (7 hits, 3 misses) and fit 4 (14 hits, 6 misses). `docs/handoff/conference-followups.md`

- **A unit level that drifts (2026-11-10).** `s_k + d_k(t)` with `d` an OU process gave +39,215 elpd over the kitchen sink on the latest split and passed the gate, where the constant unit effect of fit 4 gave +21,032 and failed it. A path per building adds another 35,565 (gate missed narrowly). `docs/notes/drift-2026-11-10.md`
- **Residual checks before a fit.** Two tables of residual correlations by time gap, at no fit cost, gave the shape of both new terms. `docs/notes/drift-2026-11-10.md`
- **Integrating a term out of the draws it slows.** theta with the unit and building levels integrated out, scales with u, levels and paths out, the building level and path in one draw, the `bld:` columns with the paths out: each took a quantity from hundreds of sweeps a draw to under 20. `docs/model/drift.md`
- **Slice directions from the step's own curvature.** Directions cut from the posterior covariance made the unit scales three times slower, because the step samples a narrower, tilted conditional. `docs/notes/drift-2026-11-10.md`

## 4. What did not work

- **Walk plus trend in one model.** Walk and trend traded off (trend scale R-hat 1.077, ESS 35). `docs/research-plan.md`
- **Coarse walks under NUTS.** The 2-year and 3-year walks failed on walk_scale, and masking or anchoring did not fix it. `docs/research-plan.md`
- **Location, transit, parks, retail and other single-feature tests.** Most scored within about 1 SE: for example parks -5.2 ± 14.3, retail +11.1 ± 15.1, lines +14.7 ± 15.8, spatial GP at 800 m +9.1 ± 14.7. In the building-zones line, transit, parks and noise were each within 3 elpd of the floor fit. `docs/model/feature-tests.md`, `docs/notes/building-zones-location-2026-10-22.md`
- **Waterfront and elevated-track columns on new buildings.** -229 (130) and -41 (51) held-out elpd. `docs/handoff/modeling.md`
- **A feature that reads earlier rents.** `nb-prevprice-v1` scored +746.9 on PSIS-LOO but only +108.9 ± 23.4 on last listings. It was blocked and removed. `docs/leak-free-scoring.md`
- **Unit-level flags from later ads.** The flags scored +60.9 ± 15.6 on rows but carried later text backward and were withdrawn. `docs/model/screen-log-2026-09-23.md`
- **Per-unit drift (m8-nocurves-2slopes).** It hit the 35-minute cap. Its drift scale mixed slowly (R-hat 1.085). `docs/research-plan.md`
- **Per-building size and second-bath slopes on combined data.** -192.1 ± 36.2 on 2026-10-02, after +1,818.7 on Chelsea. `docs/model/feature-tests.md`
- **Point-in-time MapPLUTO, first version.** `nb5-plutoasof-v1` scored -71.1 ± 20.4. `docs/model/feature-tests.md`
- **lognoise and nofloorslope.** -2,002 and -318.8. `docs/model/unadopted-work-2026-10-14.md`
- **AR(1) building deviation.** It scored +395 (lot SE 55) but failed the gate (rho R-hat 1.041, sd ESS 39), and the capped fixed-rho retry is not reported in the notes. `docs/model/arwalk-2026-10-14.md`
- **Serving over a failed gate.** The all-scraped fit failed (R-hat 1.093, ESS 11.7 at the Carlyle) and was served by Ben's decision. `config/main-analysis.json`
- **Simple B0 without location.** It failed on its damping horizon and tau, and baths absorbed the location premium (2nd full bath +62.3%, 2nd bedroom +2.7%). `docs/model/b0-baseline-2026-10-18.md`
- **500 m and 1 km surfaces in two zones.** Fitted waves were nearly collinear, and the step size fell to 0.0016 and 0.0026. `two_zone_1k.py`, `whitened_zones.py`
- **Unit-effect fit 4 on the gate.** 68 of 121 coefficients miss the gate. The cause is untested. `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof3-unitmean-2026-10-23.md`
- **The 150 m kitchen sink.** One 39.5 GiB block ran out of GPU memory. `docs/handoff/kitchen-sink.md`
- **Mundlak building means.** -25 elpd (SE 24 to 29) vs floor on held-out ads. `docs/notes/conference-followups-2026-10-22.md`
- **Splitting the stabilized discount as predicted.** 3 hits and 5 misses. `docs/notes/stabilized-split-2026-10-23.md`
- **The park placebo.** 3 hits and 8 misses; expressway and rail buildings asked more, not less. `docs/notes/park-placebo-2026-10-23.md`

## 5. Recurring lessons

- **Convergence.**
  - A fit that misses the gate is not rerun on a new seed. Check the data first, then the model structure, then the sampler (Ben, 2026-10-14). `docs/handoff/modeling.md`, `docs/model/modal.md`
  - Thin cells cause misses: studio with 3+ full baths (38 to 70 rows), `bed=2:full>=4`, buildings with a single-row unit. `docs/notes/building-zones-gibbs-2026-10-20.md`, `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof-2026-10-23.md`, `docs/handoff/modeling.md`
  - Split R-hat is set by the length of a chain, not the number of chains: its expected excess over 1 is about 1 / (2 x the effective draws in a half-chain). Pooling containers adds ESS and leaves R-hat where it was (2026-11-10). `docs/model/drift.md`
  - A pilot of 500 sweeps predicts the full fit's ESS in proportion, and it cannot see a component slower than itself. `docs/notes/drift-2026-11-10.md`
  - `amp[outer]` is among the slowest quantities in nearly every floor-line fit. `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof2-2026-10-23.md`, `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof3-cap64-2026-10-23.md`
  - Ridges between two parameters stall chains (intercept and levels; walk and season; waves on a footprint; rho and sd with correlation +0.93). The fix was a change of coordinates, not more draws. `level_walk.py`, `total_walk.py`, `whitened_zones.py`, `docs/model/arwalk-2026-10-14.md`
  - Columns fixed within a unit mix poorly once unit effects are in (fit 4). `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof3-unitmean-2026-10-23.md`
  - Short fits score too high. Scores at 300 draws sat 18 to 102 points above each design's longest run. `docs/research-plan.md`
  - Bad rows drive failures. One $1,000 row at a 5-bedroom unit held a unit effect near -2.3. `docs/model/arwalk-2026-10-14.md`
- **Leakage in scoring.**
  - A row's rent must reach the fit only as its target. A covariate built from another row's rent leaks. `docs/leak-free-scoring.md`
  - Aggregates must be as-of: later ads carried backward inflate scores (unit flags) and taking them away cost about 175 elpd in the as-of floor model. `docs/model/screen-log-2026-09-23.md`, `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof-2026-10-23.md`
  - Data rules chosen by looking at residuals are not leak-free. quarantine-v7 and v8 were not counted for that reason. `docs/leak-free-scoring.md`, `docs/model/feature-tests.md`
  - PSIS-LOO leaves the building effect in, so building-level features barely show. Use grouped holdout, explained share against a permutation null, or the new-building score. `docs/model/research-review-fable-2026-10-08.md`, `docs/model/newbuilding-2026-10-13.md`
- **Held-out splits.**
  - The latest split holds out the newest ad of units with training rows. It favors unit effects and cannot test columns for new buildings. `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof3-unitmean-2026-10-23.md`, `docs/notes/building-zones-location-2026-10-22.md`
  - The whole-building split is the test for new-building columns. `docs/notes/building-holdout-location-2026-10-22.md`
  - Removing rows reshuffles the latest split's draw. Compare across builds only on shared held-out ads (119,502 shared, in one case). `docs/notes/building-zones-floor-asof2-2026-10-23.md`, `docs/TODO.md`
  - Clustered SEs are larger than row SEs (floor over age: row 83, building 172). From 2026-10-14 the board uses the larger of iid and lot-clustered SEs. `docs/notes/conference-followups-2026-10-22.md`, `docs/handoff/modeling.md`
  - Results do not always transfer across cohorts (slopes: +1,818.7 on Chelsea, -192.1 on combined data). `docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/model/feature-tests.md`
- **GPU memory and cost.**
  - thelio's GPU slice caps at 7 GiB: baselines and short full-data fits ran out of memory. Time large changes on Modal. `docs/handoff/modeling.md`
  - The 300 m spatial GP and the 150 m kitchen sink both ran out of memory. A retry needs a memory estimate first. `docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/handoff/kitchen-sink.md`

## 6. Open questions the notes leave

- **`drift-building` through the gate (2026-11-10).** It needs chains of about 4,000 draws at 16 sweeps a draw; four chains in an hour give 1,600. Open ways: fewer chains a container, `slow_within=0.001` (0.12 s a sweep), a fit continued from a saved state. Round seven did the first and it worked for every quantity but bld_sd[outer], which misses for another reason (the next item). `docs/notes/drift-2026-11-10.md`
- **Leverage of a few buildings on a zone's spread (2026-11-10).** Under a normal prior on building levels, a building 5 to 6 spreads out moves its zone's spread by its square over 2 b N, and the sampler is slow to decide between a dear building and outlying rows, so ten buildings of placeholder listings kept `drift-building` from the gate. Leaving such buildings out of a summary of the levels does not show this, because every building with little data follows the spread: regress the spread on the buildings' squares instead. The Student-t prior (`Settings.level_dof`) removed it on one container at a held-out score 126 higher. Open: the data rule, and the pooled fits under the t prior. `docs/notes/drift-2026-11-10.md`
