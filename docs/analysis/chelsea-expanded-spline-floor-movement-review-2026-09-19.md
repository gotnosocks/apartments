# Expanded-floor fit: review of all largest movements

Reviewed **32 distinct advertisements/units and 59 own captures**, covering the
25 largest distinct-unit fitted-rent movements and all ten selected group
examples (35 selection reasons, with overlap). **No new floor correction is
established by this review.** Twenty-six cases receive a new floor proxy; six
remain unknown. All 32 previously had no analytical floor. No selected case
uses a photo-floor mask: every `expanded_floor_mask` and corresponding mask hash
is null. The five corrected-photo advertisements were reviewed separately and
are not silently treated as part of this movement panel.

Both projection stages were replayed for every case, including exact original
sidecar membership, source-row hashes, typed capture identities, raw/body hashes,
building-count evidence and description hashes. Each case was matched back to
its exact published analytical row. I read every complete own-ad description;
all repeated captures of an advertisement have identical description hashes.
No contrary numbered unit-floor claim was found. Generic “high floor” copy and
silence do not independently confirm an exact floor or physical height.

## Interpretation of the largest changes

Lantern House 1701 (5035970) moves **$36,625 → $38,050**, a $1,425 increase,
against a $49,000 ask. Its own description identifies a West-Tower junior
penthouse above the neighboring East Tower, three exposures, river views and
owner upgrades. These support further feature research; they do not establish
an exact premium or imply that the full fitted movement is a causal floor
contribution. Its remaining log residual is 0.253.

3Eleven PH405 (4861368) moves **$17,294 → $16,472**, a $822 decrease, against a
$30,000 ask. It remains floor-unknown; its penthouse/three-bedroom/view language
does not map `PH405` to floor 4 or any other number. The comparison shows a
**−0.0431 log** change in 3Eleven's common-reference building effect, and its five
largest unit-offset movements decrease **0.0569–0.0700 log**. Those five units
now have floor proxies 53, 54 or 57. The joint pattern is consistent with value
being redistributed among floor, building and unit terms. It is not a source
claim that this building became cheaper. PH405's remaining log residual grows
to 0.600, making unresolved penthouse height, area and distinctive attributes
important next research targets.

The other four selected building examples change only **−$5.85, +$2.62, −$1.38
and +$11.90** in fitted rent despite common-reference building-effect shifts of
roughly +0.022–0.024 log. Component movements can largely offset in total fitted
price. A single example is not a building-wide source adjudication.

At 3Eleven the repeated 42nd-floor sky-deck reference is a shared amenity.
At Avalon the repeated 8th-floor outdoor lounge is also communal. Neither
invalidates the new high-floor labels. Penthouse labels remain unresolved;
whole-house and duplex descriptions should not be collapsed to a single floor
without a separate, explicit representation.

## Pre-existing nonfloor issues requiring follow-up

- **Lantern House 1704, advertisement 4828991; captures 27307 and 78725.**
  Analytical values are `bathrooms=3.0`, `reported_full_bathrooms=3`,
  `reported_half_bathrooms=0`, with no bathroom-review flags. Both descriptions
  explicitly include a **powder room** and describe the second/third bedrooms'
  “en-suite jack and jill bathrooms”. Zero half baths is inconsistent with that
  powder-room claim. The exact full-bath count is ambiguous; do not simply add
  a half bath to the existing three full baths. A source-bound disputed-count
  annotation/mask is warranted for the next data revision, with exact replacement
  counts withheld pending layout/fixture evidence. This is a pre-existing bathroom
  issue and supplies no evidence against label-derived floor 17.
- **3Eleven 4407, advertisement 4982803; captures 6756 and 11013.** Both own
  descriptions say “2 MONTHS FREE ON 14 MONTH LEASE” and “Net Rent Shown.”
  The analytical target is **$9,400**, from the initial own-ad event on
  **2026-03-05**; the reviewed description contains no explicit gross amount.
  Review raw pricing and the dated own-event sequence before declaring the target
  net or replacing it. Later description wording alone cannot establish the
  basis of that earlier target. The issue is independent of the floor-44 proxy.
- **130 W17 212, advertisement 3924616; captures 37667 and 104349.** The source
  reports **three bedrooms, three full baths, zero half baths**, area unknown,
  and a **$4,500** initial ask on **2022-08-13**. Its terse description mentions
  townhouse charm, utilities included and “Marble tile bath with pedestal sink”;
  it is not a complete count inventory. The new fit remains **$10,883** with log
  residual **−0.883**. Prioritize unit identity, numeric label convention,
  bedroom/bath inventory and event-price review. Neither the residual nor singular
  prose “bath” proves an alternative count or floor, so no numeric patch follows.
- **406 W25 1FE, advertisement 1371705; captures 29643 and 84258.** Its description
  offers furnished/unfurnished accommodation and explicitly allows short or long
  terms. The historical **$2,850** ask dates to **2014-08-27**. Determine which
  term/furnishing package that target represents before scope exclusion or price
  adjustment; the text offers a long-term option too. This does not contradict
  the floor-1 label proxy.

## Bindings and limits

Selection was driven by in-sample fitted-price/component movements, not a random
sample or independent accuracy benchmark. Comparisons preserve within-fit
covariance but do not pair independent posterior draws. Expanded support also
moves the upper spline boundary knot from 52 to 57 under the fixed knot rule,
changing the induced function prior; this is documented in the matched
comparison. Review recommendations above must not be mistaken for verified
replacement data or a validation of all 6,085 newly inferred rows.
