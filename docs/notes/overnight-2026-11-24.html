2. Track A1 — Post-Gate Audit of Live c1-live-of (PR #1550, $0 Spend)

Full audit note: docs/notes/c1-live-onefit-audit-2026-11-24.md (n = 149,550 closed ≥1-yr held-out + 6,200 live open ads).

  • Kept-quantity mixing is very clean: Only 13 / 155,750 (0.008%) scored ads have pred_loc R-hat > 1.05 (0 among top live /deals), 42 / 68,008 buildings have level R-hat > 1.05, and far-thin buildings dropped from 338 → 235.
  • Live open-market weak spots (n = 6,200): Overall 80% coverage is strong on repeat units, but under-covers on bld_ads = 0 (n=87, cov80 43.7%, bias +0.188), size 1500+ (64.4%), top3_pred (64.8%), unit_ads = 0 (69.5%), and floor = unk (71.6%).
  • 140 blind NYC broker reads (3 batches): Closed high-miss batch surfaced townhouses/small lots (6 vs 1), co-ops/condos, and renovated listings; live /deals top-25 batch surfaced sparse-photo ads (≤ 1 photo, 6 vs 1) and bedroom-count mismatches.
  • Structural discoveries: (1) apply_floor_poly summed only unit:elev_f2..64, mapping elevator floor 1 and floor unknown to identical (0,0,0) coordinates (leaving a -1.39% A / -1.19% B bias in 2022+); (2) bld:coop was absent from c1-compact (co-ops were lumped into class_C/D).
Why real MCMC screening beats post-hoc residual scoring: static building/unit scale terms look huge marginally on fixed residuals, but in joint refits they trade off against unit_sd on repeat-ad units. Within-unit change signals (attr_changes) and temporal dynamics (bld_bimonthly/bld_monthly) do not confound with unit_sd and delivered real out-of-sample gains.

Why u1_size_scale & Static Scale Terms Were Dropped

  • Clean pruning: On latest, b4-attr-chg improved ELPD (+216 ± 56, z=+3.86, monotone 1.13× / 1.18× / 1.28×), MAE (0.05430 → 0.05428), RMSE (0.07820 → 0.07810), and ESS (24.0 → 29.4). By contrast, b4-u1-size slightly hurt RMSE (0.07820 → 0.07850) and b4-m-attr-u1 scored -4.1 ± 3.0 on open market — so we drop u1_size_scale and recommend the clean 2-token upgrade attr_changes + bld_monthly (or bld_bimonthly).
  • floor1_mean lesson: floor_1_flag pooled elevator floor 1 (a ground-floor penalty) with walkup floor 1 (the best, stair-free walkup floor!), canceling their opposite signs (+0.0045). An elevator-only unit:elev_floor_1 term stays queued for a future micro-screen.
  • Speed note for bld_monthly: In drift.py, CELL_PADS currently stops at 44, so any building with 45–156 active months gets padded straight to ~156. Adding intermediate pads (64, 96, 128) is a 1-line speed win before launching a full 1.9M-row c2-monthly gate fit.
