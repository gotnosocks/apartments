# H100 against H200 for the onefit fit (2026-11-23)

Full onefit fits have run on an H200. A lean large container (#1541) lists at $5.30 an hour
with an H200 and $4.71 with an H100 (`app.usd_per_second(gpu, True, True)`): 11% less. The
two cards have the same compute, and the H200 has 43% more memory bandwidth (4.8 against
3.35 TB/s) and 141 GB against 80. So the H100 is cheaper per sweep if its sweep is at most
5.30 / 4.71 = 1.125 times the H200's, and if two chains fit in its 80 GB. Earlier full-data
fits of related models swept 2 chains in 1.14 to 1.16 s on an H100 (`clean5-mkt-{a,b,c}`)
and 1.13 s on an H200 (`u5-of-a`), but those are different models.

## Probe

At 0261fede (#1542), the same model, dataset, split and shape as `mem-probe` (`2 20 20 1`,
`--large --lean --no-summary`, seed 7) on an H100, label `h100-probe`. The comparison is
`mem-probe`'s 1.610 s a sweep of 2 chains on an H200 over the same 40 sweeps. About 10
minutes, about $0.8. Not a model test.

## Predictions (written before launch)

1. Two chains fit in the H100's 80 GB and the container exits 0. P ≈ 0.8.
2. A sweep takes 1.6 to 2.2 s, central 1.8 s: ratio h ≈ 1.12 to the H200's 1.610 s.
3. Decision: if h ≤ 1.10, run the onefit fits on H100s (a margin under the 1.125
   break-even for the noise of a 40-sweep probe). Otherwise stay on H200s.
