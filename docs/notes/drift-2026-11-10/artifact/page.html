What the score cannot tell you

  • Why an ask is low. A landlord priced low, the apartment has a flaw the ad does not state, or the ad is wrong. The model sees all three as the same low ask.
  • What it rented for. The data holds asking rents, net of any free months. An ad that left the market may have rented for less.
  • Apartments it has never seen. A unit with no earlier ad is priced from its features, its building and its neighbourhood. Its range is wider, so a low ask there is weaker evidence.
  • The backtest is not fully blind. An ad's feature list comes from its latest record, which can be newer than the backtest's day. No price crosses that line.
