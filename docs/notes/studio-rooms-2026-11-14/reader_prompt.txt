You are checking NYC rental ads for a research check. Every ad here is coded as a studio (0
bedrooms), yet the listing's codes give the apartment 5 or more rooms. For each ad, find the
true bedroom count of the advertised apartment where the evidence allows it, say which
evidence settles it, and say why the room count is so high.

Read each dossier listed in BATCH with the Read tool. A dossier holds:

- the row's own ad: its coded fields, unit label, date, lister and description;
- the unit's other ads (the same StreetEasy unit page), with their coded fields and the texts
  of up to 5 of them nearest in time;
- units of the same line in the building (same letter or same last two digits on other
  floors), with their coded bedrooms, baths, rooms and sizes;
- a count of the building's other ads with the same room count, for context only;
- floor plan images already stored on disk, if any. When a dossier lists one, open the .png
  with the Read tool and look at it. Open no other image and fetch nothing from the web.

Dollar figures are masked as [amount], and no ask is given: price is never evidence here.

## What settles a count

A count of 1 bedroom or more changes a coded field, so it needs backing beyond this ad's own
words. Accept as backing:

1. `unit_other_ads`: another ad of the same unit coding that count, within about 24 months
   of the row's ad, or further away but showing the same configuration (the same text, baths,
   size or room count). An ad years away with a renovation, other baths or another layout in
   between backs nothing.
2. `floor_plan`: a stored plan you looked at that shows the walled bedrooms.
3. `line_units`: units of the same line on other floors coding that count, with a matching
   room count or size.
4. `stated_size`: a size stated in a coded field or in the text. A size alone rarely settles
   a count, since lofts and large studios run past 700 sq ft; use it together with another
   kind.

The row's own text (`own_text`) can describe the layout and support one of the four, but on
its own it is not backing for a count of 1 or more. A coded 0 that the evidence agrees with
(the text describes a studio or an open loft, and nothing else contradicts it) settles as a
studio, and there the own text may be the main evidence.

Count a bedroom when the evidence shows a separate room for sleeping, walled off from the
living space, as listers code it: a loft with built-out bedrooms counts them, while a loft or
studio with an open sleeping area, an alcove or a sleeping loft counts 0. A "convertible" or
flex room that the ad offers to wall off counts only if it is walled now.

## Verdicts

- `bedrooms`: the true count is 1 or more, with backing from the list above.
- `studio`: the true count is 0.
- `unresolved`: the evidence doesn't settle it. Say which way the row's own text leans.
- `other`: the ad is not for a single apartment (a store or office, a whole building, several
  units, a room share), so a bedroom count doesn't apply as coded.

## Why the room count is high

Pick the best of:

- `bedroom_field_left_0`: the apartment has bedrooms, and the lister or feed left the
  bedroom field at 0 while typing the rooms;
- `loft_zones`: an open loft whose room count counts zones or built-out areas;
- `studio_counts_extras`: a studio whose count includes a kitchen, foyer, alcove, dressing
  room or the like;
- `combined_or_multi_unit`: the ad covers more than one apartment or a combined unit;
- `building_or_house_count`: the count belongs to a whole house or building;
- `nonresidential`: a store, office or other nonresidential space;
- `unclear`.

## Output

Write one JSON line per dossier to OUT with the Write tool, in BATCH order, with these keys:

- `row_id`: the number in the dossier's title;
- `verdict`: "bedrooms", "studio", "unresolved" or "other";
- `true_bedrooms`: an integer, or null unless the verdict is "bedrooms" or "studio";
- `lean`: for "unresolved", "bedrooms", "studio" or "none"; otherwise "";
- `backing`: a list from "unit_other_ads", "floor_plan", "line_units", "stated_size",
  "own_text", naming each kind you relied on, or an empty list;
- `backing_detail`: at most 40 words naming the listing ids, units or plan you used and what
  they show;
- `rooms_reason`: one code from the list above;
- `quote`: at most 25 words quoted from the row's own description that matter most, or "";
- `note`: one or two plain sentences on what you found.

Open no file other than BATCH, the dossiers it lists and the plan images those dossiers list.
Then reply only with the number of lines written and the count of each verdict.
