## 4. Review Corrections & Predictions Scored (2026-11-25, PR #1554 review)

**Gate status.** The gate is split R-hat ≤ 1.01 and bulk ESS > 400 on every scalar and coefficient. `dq-c2mo-clean2` (`1.0083 / 754`) and `dq-live-c2mo-of` (`1.0085 / 680`) pass. **`dq-c2mo-tf2b` (`1.0106`) and `dq-full-c2mo-p7` (`1.0179 / 356`) fail it.** The `textflags_v2` gain (`+7.8 ± 4.5`, z ≈ 1.7, not significant) and the "best MAE" (`0.06617`) both come from failing fits. They are exploration evidence only, and `textflags_v2` has no gate-passing single-change comparison yet.

**Attribution.** `dq-live-c2mo-of` vs `c1-live-of` changes three things at once: the cleaned data (`890efa6d` vs `b157a29e`), `textflags_v2`, and `c2-monthly` in place of `c1-compact`. The `+1,058.4 ± 89.1` belongs to the bundle and can't be credited to cleaning. The paired single-change backtest suggests most of it is the `c2-monthly` structure: on the backtest, cleaning alone gave `+0.2 ± 6.1`. Section 3 calls the flag coefficients "causal"; they are model coefficients, not causal effects.
