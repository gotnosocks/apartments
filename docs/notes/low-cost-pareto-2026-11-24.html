2. Why H200 + Algorithmic Speedups Beat Cheaper GPUs by 2×

Wiring A100-40GB --lean ($2.86/h) and A100-80GB --lean ($3.26/h) into LEAN_FITS and probing full 1.76M-row sweeps proved a decisive hardware finding: once fast_scales strips away the old 40-D collapsed slice bottleneck, the sampler is pure FP64 compute + HBM3e bandwidth. 4. Four Key Engineering & Scientific Takeaways

  • Algorithmic + held-scales speedups dominate data truncation: Truncating to 2022+ ($1.13) loses -401 ± 61 ELPD and degrades causal βr to 0.896 (flipping unit:log_size_rel and inflating missing-building-attribute coefficients). Keeping all 13 years with --scales-fixed c2mo-j costs almost the same ($1.40–$2.30) while scoring +445 ELPD higher with βr = 0.9993 and 97/100 deal overlap!
  • Exact Fisher-preconditioned HMC + intermediate CELL_PADS collapsed free-scales c2-monthly from $20–$23 → $4.61: Pulling 39 noise factors out of collapsed update_scales and adding intermediate CELL_PADS = (..., 60, 80, 108, 144) cut bld_monthly's overhead over quarterly from +89% to +17%.
  • Mechanistic mixing discovery (fast_scales_p6): On free-scales pf-full-c2mo ($4.61), every non-phase parameter passed (min ESS = 499); only phase:none / unit_sd / sigma missed because scales_no_phase separated the 6 lease-gap factors from collapsed update_scales. Keeping just those 6 lease-gap factors in update_scales (fast_scales_p6, K=10 instead of K=43) delivered a 2×–5.5× ESS jump on local GPU A/B testing for only +23% sweep time.
  • Localized quarterly segment knots (seg_add) beat global eigenmodes (seg_add_lr12) by 10× on mixing: While pf-c2mo-lr12-fix ($1.27) retained 10,257.5 ELPD and 0.06706 MAE, its global Matérn eigenmodes coupled strongly with the 13-year building AR(1) paths (R-hat = 1.054, ESS = 51). Localized seg_add passed the 4-chain gate cleanly at $2.29 (pf-c2mo-fix-gate, R-hat = 1.0089, ESS = 535) — keep seg_add in production!
