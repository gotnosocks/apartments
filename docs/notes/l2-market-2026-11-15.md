# L2 on the market of 2026-11-09, for Deals

Deals and Price now come from round nine's `drift-building` fits: `n9` on the market split,
`j9` on the backtest and `b9` on the latest split. L2 (`drift-building-phase-unitkn-level`,
`docs/notes/l2-unitkn-level-2026-11-14.md`) passed the gate on the latest split. I scored it
against b9 on the 189,647 held-out ads both runs share with
`l2-market-2026-11-15/cmp.py`, clustering the SE by building:

- L2 leads by 6,442 elpd, SE 227, about 28 SE, and by 6,214 after a constant shift of each;
- L2 puts 2,166 asks below its 1st percentile, b9 1,302.

The lead belongs to the whole line from b9 to L2: the unit record, the Student-t unit level,
the phase terms and L2's level term, which gained 520 of it on its own. It is far past the 2 SE
of the serving rule, so I fit L2 on the two datasets Deals needs and publish from them if they
pass.

L2 also differs from b9 in its data: it was fit on the closing-raise rows of the 2026-10-23
build. The Deals fits use the closing-raise copies of round nine's datasets, from the build
of 2026-11-09, so the list stays the market of that day:

- market: `b0-ddd2fe8f5da041b8-alldata-dense-nyc-market-closingraise`, 1,893,502 training
  rows and the 6,774 open ads, 9 of them repriced by the closing-raise rule;
- backtest: `b0-ddd2fe8f5da041b8-alldata-dense-nyc-asof-20251110-closingraise`, the build
  as of 2025-11-10 with its 9,346 open ads, 440 of them repriced by the rule.

The `inputs.json` of each dataset points at the same snapshots as n9's and j9's, and no run has used
either yet.

## Launch plan

Six H100 containers, two pools of three with seeds 1 to 3, each pooled by `drift_pool` into 6
chains, from `/data1/apartments/serve/master` at the merge commit of this note. L2's settings
carry over unchanged. Container `l2m-a` starts alone, and the other five follow once its log
has passed "synced" and its first sweeps, so a fault in the new datasets costs one container.

```sh
IN=/data1/apartments/pipeline/datasets/rows-ddd2fe8f5da041b8.parquet
DS=/data1/apartments/frontier/datasets/b0-ddd2fe8f5da041b8-alldata-dense-nyc
ops/modal-fit --outside-budget --gpu H100 --dataset "$DS-market-closingraise" --input "$IN" \
  --split market --baseline drift-building-b0-market-67d14e17-n9 COMMIT l2m-a \
  drift-building-phase-unitkn-level b0 2 500 9000 40 --seed 1 --level-dof 4 \
  --no-loo --no-heldout
ops/modal-fit --outside-budget --gpu H100 --dataset "$DS-asof-20251110-closingraise" --input "$IN" \
  --split market --baseline drift-building-b0-market-67d14e17-j9 COMMIT l2j-a \
  drift-building-phase-unitkn-level b0 2 500 9000 40 --seed 1 --level-dof 4 \
  --no-loo --no-heldout
```

with `l2m-b`, `l2m-c`, `l2j-b` and `l2j-c` on seeds 2 and 3. L2's three containers cost $11.06
on 1.71 million training rows. The market pool has 11% more rows and the backtest pool 3%
more, so I expect about $12 a pool. With Price now's CPU container, about $1.27, the sprint
goes from $21.88 to about $47 of $100.

## Predictions (written before launch)

1. The market pool passes the gate (80%), and so does the backtest pool (80%). L2's own pool
   passed at R-hat 1.0050 and bulk ESS 1,141. A miss, if one comes, is on `unit_sd` or a
   building spread (70% given a miss).
2. On the backtest's 9,346 ads scored at their final asks (`drift_deals validate`), elpd
   beats j9's 9,979 (75%), by 100 to 600 (50%).
3. The backtest's median absolute error at the final ask moves from j9's 0.0474 by under
   0.002 (75%), and its 80% coverage lies between 78% and 81% (70%).
4. In the backtest's bottom tenth by first-ask place, the median days on market stays at or
   under j9's 26 (60%), and the share raised later stays at or under j9's 37% (55%).
5. On the market list, more ads have a below share under 0.1 than the 564 of n9 (65%),
   between 600 and 900 (45%). On the held-out ads, L2 put 2,166 asks below its 1st
   percentile against b9's 1,302, so I expect the same heavier lower tail here.
6. Of n9's 100 ads with the lowest below share, at least 60 stay among L2's lowest 100 (60%).
7. Each pool costs between $11.00 and $13.00 (80%).

## Accepting it

I publish Deals from the market pool with the backtest pool if both pass the gate (split
R-hat at most 1.01 and bulk ESS above 400 on every scalar and coefficient) and the backtest's
elpd at the final ask is no worse than j9's by more than 2 SE, with the SE of the paired
difference clustered by building. I publish to a
staging folder first and compare it with the served list. Price now follows from the market
pool through `ops/modal/today.py`. If either pool misses the gate, I read the data, then the
model, then the sampler, as `CLAUDE.md` asks, and the served list stays as it is.

## Results

The six containers ran from 22:47 and 23:15 ET on Nov 15 and all exited cleanly. Modal put them
at $3.50 to $3.62 each: $10.60 for the market pool and $10.74 for the backtest pool, so the
sprint stands at $43.22 of $100. I pooled each set with `drift_pool`:

| Pool | Chains x draws | Max split R-hat | Min bulk ESS | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| backtest, `...-l2j` | 6 x 2,640 | 1.0067 (`drift_sd`) | 1,223 (`unit_sd`) | pass |
| market, `...-l2m` | 6 x 2,360 | 1.0108 (`phase_scale[1]`) | 904 (`unit_sd`) | miss |

The market pool misses on `phase_scale[1]` by 0.0008, so Deals and Price now stay on n9 and j9.

The data and the model account for the miss:

- `phase_scale[1]` scales the noise of the `none` phase, a unit's first ad or a row with no
  unit. Its draws center on 1.523 with a spread of 0.010, and the six chain means differ by at
  most 0.004.
- Within a chain it mixes well: autocorrelation is 0.11 at lag 10 and gone by lag 50, and its
  bulk ESS is 1,376. The R-hat comes from the chains' means, mostly the first chain's first quarter,
  at 1.520 against 1.522 to 1.527 for its later quarters.
- Across draws it correlates with every group's `sigma` at -0.70 to -0.80, and with the other
  phase factors at 0.51 to 0.71. The noise of a row is a group's sigma times its phase factor,
  and only the `10-13` phase is fixed at 1, so sigma and the factors trade off along a ridge.
  `sigma[1]` (1.0090) and `sigma[2]` (1.0083) come close to the gate for the same reason.
- The market dataset holds 11% more rows than L2's own, most of them recent first ads in the
  `none` phase. The backtest pool, cut a year earlier, passes with the same model.

I first read the ridge as the cause. On the pooled traces, the ridge mixes well:

- `update_scales` already draws sigma and the phase factors together with `unit_sd`. It
  integrates out u, the buildings' levels and the paths, and it slices along directions that
  warmup resets twice from the step's own curvature. Every reset in all three containers found
  that curvature negative definite.
- Along the ridge itself, the mean log phase factor minus the mean log sigma, split R-hat is
  1.0074 and bulk ESS 1,624.
- The slow quantity is the noise of group 1's `none` rows,
  `log sigma[1] + log phase_scale[1]`, with R-hat 1.0107 and bulk ESS 909. Its chain means run
  from -3.7613 to -3.7623, a spread of 0.1% in the noise sd. By container mean, `l2m-a` is
  highest and `l2m-c` lowest.
- Within a chain, that noise correlates with `log unit_sd` at -0.27. Since `unit_sd` moves in
  the same step, this coupling is already sampled jointly.

The scale step holds each unit's Student-t mixing scale fixed while it moves the noise, with
`unit_dof` fixed at 3 in this run. Because a unit's first ad carries both the unit's level and
the row's noise, my guess is that these per-unit scales are what slows the first-ad noise. The
-0.27 does not test that guess. I'll check it on a subset fit before any change.
