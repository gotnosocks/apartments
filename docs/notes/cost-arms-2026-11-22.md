# Cost arms: coarser building paths and no slow step (2026-11-22)

Aim: a cheaper full fit with as much of the held-out score as possible. These arms are the
sampler's structure, not feature blocks; the simplify thread
(`docs/notes/simplify-model-discovery-2026-11-22.md` on its branch) covers the blocks.

Model `drift-building-phase-unitkn-cost` (`rentfrontier.drift_building_phase_unitkn_cost`):
the ablate model with extra `--variant` tokens.

- `bld_semi`: the buildings' AR(1) paths in half-years (26 cells at most, from 52).
- `bld_annual`: the same in years (13 cells).
- `no_slow`: no `update_slow` step; the model is unchanged.

Every arm: `--variant layrng,size,sv_gp_mo,marks,<arm>` on `onefit-c-k5` (held out = open
ads ∪ the latest split of closed ads in units with no open ad;
`docs/notes/one-fit-protocol-2026-11-22.md`), screen settings `2 250 400 10`, seed 1.
Anchor: `of-c-both` (the same variant on commit 1d62c667, no cost token). Each arm makes one
change against the anchor.

## Predictions (written before launch)

Latest-row elpd at k5 (multiply by about 5 for the full data), sampling s/sweep, and the
gate quantities' min ESS, against the anchor:

| arm | Δ elpd, latest rows | Δ elpd, open ads | s/sweep | min ESS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bld_semi` | −40 to +10 | −10 to +5 | −10% to −25% | within ±30% |
| `bld_annual` | −150 to −20 | −30 to 0 | −20% to −35% | within ±30% |
| `no_slow` | −15 to +15 (same model) | −5 to +5 | −5% to −15% | may fall on `bld:` |

A building path arm is worth a full fit if it keeps at least 95% of the anchor's latest-row
score within noise (Δ > −2 SE at k5) and cuts s/sweep by 10% or more.
