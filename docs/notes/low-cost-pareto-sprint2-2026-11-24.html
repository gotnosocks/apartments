2. Where the Time Went: Crushing Prepare (191s → 3.2s), Compile (77s → 9.1s), and Mixing

Three orthogonal systems & algorithmic changes drove Sprint 2's collapse: (1) persistent /vol/b0-design-cache cut CPU pandas prep on GPU containers from 191s → 3.2–4.2s; (2) vectorizing slow_system and collapsing seg:zone:outer:* + flag:* with building_ar jumped worst-parameter ESS 6.2× while holding amp/gp (c2mo-j-all) actually sped up sweeps (0.416 → 0.373 s/sw); (3) 15-step deterministic variational-EM warm-start (em_warm, 2.5s) lets 2-chain exact Gibbs hit 10,260.6 ELPD in 83 seconds.

3. Why 50¢ EM-Warm Exact Gibbs Beats 45¢ EM-Laplace by +142 ELPD

Deterministic variational-EM + stochastic-Gamma Laplace (pf2-em-c2mo, $0.45, 4ch PASS) beat exact Gibbs on the median apartment (median ΔELPD = +0.0046) and beat $2.04 range-j by +76.7 ELPD, but lost -145.5 ELPD on ~200 thin/outlier buildings where the joint Student-t posterior over (lam, kap, ukap) is multimodal so a single EM mode narrows 80% intervals 2–2.7×. Using just 15 EM iterations as a 2.5-second warm-start into exact Gibbs (pf2-em-gibbs50, $0.53) recovers 100% of those tails.

4. Deals Backtest Validation (DOM & Price-Cut Monotonicity)

Every tier on the new frontier—from $0.45 EM-Laplace and $0.53 EM-warm Gibbs to $1.26 4ch PASS—preserves crisp, monotone Deals validation: D1 ads lease in 27–29 days with 15.9–17.0% price cuts, while overpriced D10 ads sit 71–72 days with 62.1–63.3% price cuts and causal βr ≥ 0.9992.
