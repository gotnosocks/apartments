1. Single-Fit Consolidation Architecture (Eliminating 2×–3× Fit Multiplication)

  • One unified model everywhere (c1-compact): Retires the fork between u5-svgpmo-lat (with 7-GP seg) and u5-svgpmo-mkt (no seg), plus legacy range-m3/b9/n9/m7.
  • --split unified (2% non-active closed holdout + all open listings): Keeps 100% of every active unit's own closed history and 98% of neighbor closed comps in training, while yielding ~35,000 zero-leakage historical held-out ads + all active ads from one single fit.
  • All 4 site publishers upgraded: drift_deals, drift_estimates, drift_diagnostics, and drift_today (now supporting continuous sv_gp* and additive seg_add/seg_add_semi) all run directly off one unified run.
  • --scales-fixed RUN_DIR for unitkn/ablate: Any converged unified base fit can power 15–20 min, ~$2 weekly Deals refreshes and historical backtests.
