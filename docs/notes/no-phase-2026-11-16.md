# L2 without the lease phases, checked on 1 building in 50

Ben asked on Nov 16 whether the lease phases should come out of the model. They are the
only terms reading the dates of a unit's other ads, and he judges their gain small for the
complexity they add. This note collects the evidence on hand and sets up a free check on
thelio's GPU.

## Evidence on hand

L2 (`drift-building-phase-unitkn-level`) leads b9 by 6,442 elpd on the 189,647 held-out ads
of the latest split. By the records of each step:

| step | elpd gained | source |
|---|---|---|
| Student-t unit level, rows' nu at 2 (L1 to L1TKN) | 4,106 | `l1-row-dof-fixed-2026-11-13.md` |
| noise factor per phase (c3ab to L1) | about 610: +837 on ads from 2023, -227 on 2020 to 2022 | `year-noise-2026-11-12.md` |
| level per phase (L1TKN to L2) | 520, of it 419 on ads within 2 months of the previous ad | `l2-unitkn-level-2026-11-14.md` |

So the phases account for about 1,100 of the 6,442, near 0.006 an ad. No fit has measured them
on the Student-t base, since both phase steps were measured against a phased or a normal
base.

The phases cost 12 parameters (6 noise factors, 6 levels). L2's noise factors are 1.52 for a
unit's first ad, 1.59 at 3 to 9 months, about 1.30 at 14 to 21 and past 26, and 1.04 to 1.09
elsewhere, all against 10 to 13 months. The market pool of Nov 15 missed the gate on the
first-ad factor alone (R-hat 1.0108), which kept L2 off Deals. On the backtest's final asks
L2 ties j9 (9,980.7 against 9,978.6), and its 80% coverage is 77.5% against j9's 79.2%.

## The check

`drift-building-unitkn` (`rentfrontier.drift_building_unitkn`) is L1TKN's sweep on the
building model's design: the Student-t unit level at 3 degrees of freedom, the rows' nu at
2, no noise factors and no phase levels. I run it and L2 on the k50 building subsample
(41,576 training rows, 4,649 held-out ads) with the rehearsals' settings, one after the
other, and score the held-out ads of both with `drift_score`:

```sh
systemd-run --user --unit=frontier-nophase-k50 -p MemoryMax=10G \
  ops/job gpu docs/notes/rehearsals-2026-11-14/rehearse.sh \
  drift-building-unitkn k50-r8/base nophase-k50 500 2400 nophase-k50
systemd-run --user --unit=frontier-l2-k50 -p MemoryMax=10G \
  ops/job gpu docs/notes/rehearsals-2026-11-14/rehearse.sh \
  drift-building-phase-unitkn-level k50-r8/base l2-k50 500 2400 l2-k50
```

Each run takes about 20 minutes. Neither is a full fit: at k50 the earlier rehearsals left
`unit_sd` far from the gate, so I read the scores as a comparison of two short chains on the
same rows, with the paired difference clustered by building.

## Predictions (written before the runs)

At 0.006 an ad the full data's gain scales to about 28 elpd on 4,649 ads, and the per-ad
spreads of the two full-data steps put the SE near 10.

1. L2 leads the phase-free model on held-out elpd (90%), by 10 to 50 (60%).
2. The median absolute error of the two differs by under 0.0005 (75%).
3. On held-out ads 3 to 9 months after the unit's previous ad, the phase-free model's 80%
   interval covers fewer asks than L2's (70%). Over all ads the two 80% coverages differ by
   under 1 point (70%).
4. Deal order moves little: the Spearman correlation of the two models' cdf at the ask is
   above 0.97 (70%), and at least 80% of the ads in L2's lowest tenth by cdf are in the
   phase-free model's lowest tenth (65%).
5. Among ads in the lowest tenth of one model only, first ads make up a larger share of
   those the phase-free model adds than of those it drops (60%): with a single sigma, a
   first ad's interval is narrower than L2's, so a low first ask sits further into the
   tail.
6. The phase-free run's lowest bulk ESS over sigma is above the lowest of L2's sums
   log sigma[g] + log first-ad factor (65%), and its sampling time is below L2's by under
   10% (60%).

## Reading the result

The k50 runs measure the phases' size on the Student-t base and the share of deal candidates
they move. A full-data answer needs a pool of `drift-building-unitkn` on the latest split,
about $11, scored against L2's pool. I launch that only on Ben's word, since it tests a
removal he proposed and its result changes the list of deals only through his choice between
the two models.

buildings, and compared them with `no-phase-2026-11-16/cmp.py`, clustering the SE by building.

| 50 / 80 / 90 / 95% coverage | 53.3 / 81.0 / 89.9 / 95.2% | 52.2 / 80.4 / 89.7 / 94.5% |

| 10-13 | 776 | 84.8% | 82.0% | 10.4 |

The carried split holds out ads of units with an earlier ad, so none of the 4,649 is a first

The two models order the ads almost alike. The Spearman correlation of their cdfs at the ask
is 0.993, and 436 of the 464 ads in L2's lowest tenth are in the phase-free model's lowest
tenth, which is 94%.

For the sampler, the phase-free run's bulk ESS on log sigma runs from 400 to 620 over the
four groups. L2's runs from 496 to 632 on log sigma, and from 319 to 424 on a group's log
sigma plus the log first-ad factor.

Prediction scores:

1. Right on both parts: L2 leads, by 44.
2. Right: the median absolute errors differ by 0.0002.
3. Right on both parts: at 3 to 9 months the phase-free interval covers 77.2% against L2's
   81.0%, and over all ads the coverages differ by 0.5 points.
4. Right on both parts, with more room than I gave it: 0.993 and 94%.
5. Not scored. The held-out ads of this split include no first ad.
6. Right on ESS (400 against 319). Wrong on time: the phase-free run sampled 16% faster,
   where I expected under 10%.

## Reading

On this subsample the phases are worth 0.009 elpd an ad and move 6% of the lowest tenth. At
that rate the full latest split's 189,647 ads would give L2 a lead near 1,800, above the
1,100 the earlier records sum to. This is an estimate of size from two runs short of the
gate, on held-out ads with no first ad among them. A verdict under the serving rule needs
the full pool. The phases cost 16% of sampling time, the `phase_scale[1]` miss of
the market pool, and a term that depends on time since the unit's previous ad.

The lead splits into parts with different uses:

- The noise factors change interval widths by phase. A deal list ranked by cdf feels them
  where the phase-free intervals are wrong by 3 to 4 points of coverage, at 3 to 9 and at
  10 to 13 months.
- The phase levels shift the median by phase. L2's level term gained 520 elpd on the full
  data.
- First ads, where the full-data factor is 1.52, are absent from this check. The market
  list is mostly first ads or ads of units with a long gap, so a deal list would feel the
  first-ad factor more than this subsample shows.

A model between the two would keep a single factor for first ads and drop the other five
factors and the six levels. This check does not measure it. The full-data answer for the
phase-free model still needs the $11 pool on the latest split.

## Full data, as a short fit

Ben approved the full-data fit on Nov 16. The short-fit test of
`short-chains-2026-11-16.md` passed, so I run it as a single container with 500 warmup
sweeps and 320 kept sweeps of 2 chains, and compare it with L2's short fit `l2short` of
the same settings and seed (held-out elpd 237,341.6):

```sh
D=/data1/apartments/frontier/datasets/b0-b3ddebf19af1dc1e-alldata-v36-dense-nyc-closingraise
IN=/data1/apartments/pipeline/datasets/rows-b3ddebf19af1dc1e.parquet
ops/modal-fit --outside-budget --gpu H100 --dataset "$D" --input "$IN" --split latest \
  --baseline drift-building-phase-unitkn-level-b0-latest-75fe7f29-l2short COMMIT nophase-short \
  drift-building-unitkn b0 2 500 320 1 --seed 1 --level-dof 4 \
  --no-loo --no-heldout
```

Two short fits can differ by about 60 elpd from sampling noise alone. Both miss the scalar
gate by construction, so the result sizes the phases and a decision to serve the
phase-free model would still need its pool.

Predictions, written before the launch:

1. L2's short fit leads on held-out elpd (95%), by 1,000 to 2,200 (65%).
2. The 80% coverages of the two over all held-out ads differ by under 0.6 points (70%). On
   ads 3 to 9 months after the unit's previous ad, the phase-free interval covers at least
   2 points less than L2's (70%), with phases assigned as in `no-phase-2026-11-16/cmp.py`.
3. The Spearman correlation of the two cdfs at the ask is above 0.99 (70%), and at least
   90% of the ads in L2's lowest tenth are in the phase-free model's lowest tenth (70%).
4. The median absolute errors differ by under 0.0005 (75%).
5. The container settles between $1.00 and $1.60 (75%).
