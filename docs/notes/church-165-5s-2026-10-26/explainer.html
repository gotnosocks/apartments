What could move this

  • The $13,500 dates from the day the listing went into contract. It may be the agreed rent rather than an ask that tested the market. The model treats it as the closing ask and dates it to the first day of the listing.
  • Bedrooms carry most of the premium (×1.82). Every 5-bed in the building lists 2,000 to 2,200 sq ft and 9 rooms, about 400 sq ft a bedroom, so a miscoded count would move the price more than anything else. The ad mentions bay windows in each bedroom. Nothing was changed.
  • The ad leaves the elevator blank, which costs 1%. PLUTO codes the building D7, an elevator building.
  • The building's own level and walk are the widest terms. With 13 rows over 12 years, both 95% ranges include zero, and together they take about 15% off what Tribeca and the unit's features alone would give.
  • A DOB alteration certificate is dated 28 April 2025, a day before the listing went up. The renovation may be worth more than the "renovated" text flag captures.
