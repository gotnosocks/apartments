2. The 52-Quarter Volatility Trajectory: Smooth GP vs Discrete Steps vs Causal Speed

The chart below plots the actual posterior noise-scale multipliers across all 52 quarters (2014 Q1 – 2026 Q4) from vg-lat-2-svgp, vg-lat-1-y1cov, and vg-lat-3-velgp. Because drift.py natively supports non-binary additive phase_map matrices with PHASE_PRIOR = 0.3, sv_gp injects the exact whitened Matérn-3/2 eigenbasis (α = 0.25, ℓ = 1.0 yr, 22 orthogonal modes covering >99% of prior variance, anchored at 2024 Q1 = 1.00×) with zero changes to the sampler.

Why sv_gp Crushes y1 + covid_vol

  • No Jan 1, 2020 cliff: Discrete yr:2020 slams calm pre-lockdown Jan–Feb 2020 with 1.58× noise; sv_gp starts 2020 Q1 at 1.04× and ramps smoothly through 1.33× (Q2) and 1.73× (Q3) to a true 2.01× peak in 2020 Q4.
  • No Jan 1, 2022 drop-off: Instead of holding all of 2021 at 1.52× and dropping abruptly to 1.13×, sv_gp decays smoothly from 1.97× (2021 Q1) through 1.66× → 1.36× → 1.19×, then captures the 2022 boom wave (1.15× → 1.19×).
  • Zero external data dependencies: Retires the NYC DOHMH Covid CSV completely while outperforming it by +275.4 ELPD.

What 2-Parameter vel_gp Proves

  • Strong asymmetric crash physics: Posterior multipliers are vel:down = 1.264× (log = +0.234 ± 0.005, per −15%/yr drop) vs vel:up = 1.094× (log = +0.090 ± 0.005, per +15%/yr rise) — falling markets inflate dispersion 2.6× more per %/yr than booms.
  • 92.8% of time-vol lift in 2 numbers: vel_gp recovers +1,354.8 of +1,459.6 ELPD and matches sv_gp in 2020 (+138.2 vs +139.0 over y1+covid_vol) while running −17% faster.
  • Why sv_gp still wins cleanly: Un-deseasonalized weekly asks dip every Q4 winter, creating spurious 1.14×–1.31× Q4 bumps in vel_gp; adding vel_gp on top of sv_gp adds only +38.7 ± 34.8 (z = +1.11) while halving phase_factor ESS (68 → 32).
