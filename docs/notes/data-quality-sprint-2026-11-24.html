Notable Systemic Discoveries Fixed in This Sprint

  • half_baths ≥ 3 decimal-parse corruption (288 peer-backed repairs + 6 in pkt3): The CPU benchmark flagged baths_3p as 4.77× enriched in worst misses. Scanning all 1.9M rows revealed 605 listings with half_baths ≥ 3 (e.g., 1.5 baths imported as full_baths=1, half_baths=5 → total_baths=3.5!). Repairing all 288 same-unit same-bedroom unanimous/supermajority peer-backed rows (plus 6 in pkt3) restores exact bath counts without dropping real rental observations.
  • 70-pine-street 28-row feed-batch corruption (4372876–4373067 → 56 set entries): Auditing extreme outliers exposed a contiguous feed dump where 28 genuine studios, 1BRs, and 2BRs ("Starting at $3,825–$9,531") were stamped 4.0br / 3 full / 1 half bath. Every single one of the 28 rows had 100% unanimous same-unit peers, letting us repair both bedrooms and bathrooms across all 28 rows rather than discarding them.
  • Bidirectional cross-borough street-name misjoins (94 excludes in sprint + 10 queued): Alongside classic Manhattan-slug ← Brooklyn-apartment collisions (824-lexington-avenue-new_york, 112–140-manhattan-avenue-new_york, 299–2318-broadway-new_york, 25-bleecker-street-new_york), our reverse scan uncovered Manhattan apartments (West Village, Greenwich Village, Tribeca, Murray Hill) misjoined onto Brooklyn slugs (12-cornelia-street-brooklyn, 19-park-place-brooklyn, 500-6-avenue-brooklyn, 591-3-avenue-brooklyn, 148-bleecker-street-brooklyn, 297-wilson-avenue-brooklyn).
  • Broker delist deterrent bumps, 2× asks & placeholders (62 excludes): Caught $9,999/$8,888/$5,555 placeholders, exact 2.00× doubled asks (2179890, 2881216, 1956134, 2978503, 4345650), unit numbers entered as rent (2727887: unit 2501 → $2,501), and 208-east-95-street / 259-west-30-street off-market "delisted" / "not avail" price spikes.
