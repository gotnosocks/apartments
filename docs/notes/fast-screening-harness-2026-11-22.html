Fast Screening Harness on Modal Cloud GPUs (2026-11-22)

Goal: Maximize information gain per unit time (and per dollar) when testing new apartment-rent model features and structural upgrades, by building, benchmarking, and calibrating a reusable Modal Cloud GPU Fast Screening Harness (rentfrontier.screen, ops/modal-screen, and a persistent XLA compilation cache on /vol/jax-cache).

Scope & Budget: Targeted strictly at Modal cloud GPUs (L4, A100-40GB, A100-80GB, H100) rather than thelio's local RTX 2060. Sampler speedups (double-pass unit/scale sweep, tracing 24 phase factors instead of 1,905 cells, slow_within) were deliberately deferred so this study isolates pure screening-harness leverage on the current sampler as-is. Authorized Modal spend ceiling: $50.00; actual spend across 46 settled Modal containers: $19.11 ($30.89 remaining).

Executive Summary

  • Modal GPU Pareto winner — A100-40GB + persistent /vol/jax-cache: Warm screens run l2bt (2 chains, 250 warmup, 400 draws) in 5.4 min ($0.23) on 10% of buildings (k10) and 6.5 min ($0.27) on 20% of buildings (k5) — 9–11× faster wall clock and 55–65× cheaper than a full 6-chain Modal fit (~60–90 min, ~$15–$18).
  • Persistent XLA cache cuts compile time by −76%: First-sweep compile drops from 104.3s → 24.9s at k10, 107.5s → 26.1s at k5, and 112.6s → 26.6s on unified-seg, reusing compiled executables even across different draw counts.
  • Cloud-GPU fixed-overhead surprise (k5 beats k20): Because container staging, design prep, and kernel launch overhead dominate small datasets on an A100, quadrupling the dataset from 5% (k20, 94k rows) to 20% (k5, 376k rows) adds only +1.1 min / +$0.04 warm over k10 while halving sampling SE / MDE and cutting Gaussian-process attenuation from −27% to −12%.
  • 18 / 18 perfect 3-way routing (GO / KILL / ESCALATE): Zero false promotions on the −63 negative control (d1 - b9), 12/12 promotions on every ≥+500 ELPD upgrade, and zero false kills on the borderline +224 feature (l2bt - l2kn). On the asof-20251110 Deals backtest (market split, k5, $0.78), the screen projected +159 ± 83 (true +173.8) and automatically flagged the exact −1.90 pp 80% coverage regression.

Plain-English Glossary of Terms & Abbreviations

Every metric and shorthand used in the tables and charts below:

Term / CodePlain-English Meaning
ELPD & ΔELPDExpected Log Predictive Density summed across held-out apartment ads. Higher is better: ΔELPD = candidate − baseline measures how much more probable the candidate model makes the true held-out asking rents.
SE & zBuilding-clustered standard error of paired ΔELPD (accounting for correlation among units inside the same building) and its signal-to-noise ratio \(z = \Delta\text{ELPD} / \text{SE}\).
k20, k10, k5Nested, building-size-decile stratified subsamples keeping 1 in 20 (5%), 1 in 10 (10%), or 1 in 5 (20%) of all NYC buildings. All rows of each chosen building stay intact, and carried_heldout carries the exact full-data held-out mask with zero leakage.
Oracle vs Fit / OraOracle scores the full-data fitted models on the exact same subsample buildings. Comparing Fit / Oracle cleanly separates irreducible building-draw luck from small-sample parameter shrinkage ("attenuation").
MDE (80%)Minimum Detectable Effect at 80% statistical power and 2-sided \(|z| \ge 1.96\): \(2.80 \times \text{SE}_{\text{full}}\). Smaller MDE means the screen can resolve finer improvements.
IG (bits), bits/min, bits/$Continuous Bayesian Shannon information gain \(\frac{1}{2}\log_2(1 + \tau_0^2/\sigma_{\text{obs}}^2)\) under a \(\mathcal{N}(0, 1500^2)\) prior on full-data ΔELPD, divided by screen wall minutes or settled Modal dollars.
R-hat & ESSMCMC convergence diagnostics across chains: R-hat near 1.00 means chains agree; ESS is Effective Sample Size of independent draws. Full publishes require R-hat ≤ 1.05; fast screens allow up to 1.15 with warnings above 1.05.
GO / KILL / ESCALATE3-way screening decision: GO promote straight to a full Modal fit; KILL drop cheaply; ESCALATE inconclusive (typically +100..+300 ELPD), step up to 4 chains or larger f.
XLA cacheJAX's persistent compiled GPU kernel cache stored on Modal's shared volume (/vol/jax-cache), skipping ~80 seconds of cold compilation on repeat runs of the same model and dataset shape.

4. Experiment 3 — Draw Budgets, 4-Chain Batching, Seed-2 & market Screen

  • Draw-budget stability (screen-k10): Moving from 2 250 400 10 (650 sweeps) to 2 350 800 20 (1,150 sweeps) shifted l2bt − l2kn by only 0.1 ELPD (+3.1 → +3.2) and u4s − l2bt by −1.8% (+4,628 → +4,545, warm compile 26.6s, R-hat = 1.029). Warmup below 250 sweeps (150) broke slice adaptation (R-hat = 1.45).
  • Vectorized 4-chain batching (--chains 4 in 1 GPU batch): On k10 d1 − b9, 4 chains flipped the noisy 2-chain estimate to the true negative sign (+13 ± 23 → −12 ± 18) and cut paired SE by 23% for +28s wall time. On k5, 4 chains delivered R-hat = 1.047–1.052, ESS = 99–100 in ~9.2 min warm (~$0.39).
  • 2nd independent seed (screen-k10-s2): Disjoint 10% building samples agreed to 4 ELPD on u1 − l2bt (+2,940 ± 453 vs +2,944 ± 504, both GO), while l2bt − l2kn tracked its +236 oracle (+186 ± 132).
  • market asof-20251110 Deals backtest screen (screen-mkt-k5, 1,891 open ads, $0.78): unified vs range-j projected +159 ± 83 (z = +1.92, P(>0) = 0.973, P(>50) = 0.906) against +173.8 full truth — and automatically emitted a coverage-regression warning (80% coverage 76.89% → 74.99%, −1.90 pp; <5% tail 4.02% → 4.39%), matching the exact −2.5 pp regression seen on the full $15 backtest.
