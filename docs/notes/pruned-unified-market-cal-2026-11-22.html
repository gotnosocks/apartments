Plain-English Takeaway: We made the unified rent model ~40% faster and cheaper by tracing only its 24 underlying noise multipliers instead of 1,905 combinations; proved that dropping weak features (bedunit, sbf) and adding lagged NYC Covid volatility + y1_pre2024 beats our current full-data champion (u4s-lat) by +291 ELPD; discovered that mark_baths alone drives the market mark gain while mark_changed and mark_large01br hurt; and validated a leak-free 1.12× open-market uncertainty scale completely out-of-sample across two independent market dates 12 months apart.

1. 24-Factor Trace Speedup (drift.py)

Mapped scale designs (unified, unified-seg, and mapped ablate variants) previously expanded all P ≤ 1,905 observed factor-combination cells at every MCMC draw and ran split R-hat/ESS over all 1,905 cells. Tracing only the F ≤ 24 free phase_factor values and expanding kept/phase_scale only on kept draws cut screening wall time and Modal cost by 39–45% with bit-for-bit identical predictive scale expansion.

4. Two Independent Market Dates & Out-of-Sample open_scale Calibration

Root-cause diagnosis on full u3-mkt (n = 9,346) showed that open asks have 1.076× wider dispersion than 2025 closed ads even on re-listed apartments (1.099× overall, 1.161× on first ads). Older models hid this gap because 2020–2022 noise inflated global sigma_g. Once y1/covid_vol isolated 2020–2022, closed-ad sigma_2025 dropped to the true closed-ad floor, exposing the open-ad gap. Because training rows are strictly closed (open == False), an open-market multiplier cannot be learned inside the closed-ad fit—it must be calibrated out-of-sample on open asks.

Calibrated on full u3-mkt (2025-11-10, where 1.12× hit exact 80.00% coverage and +33.50 ELPD), we tested drift_score --open-scale completely out-of-sample on a second independent market snapshot 12 months later (screen-mkt2-k5, 2026-11-20, n = 1,280 live asks). Both dates track almost identically:

7. Plain-English Glossary

  • ELPD (Expected Log Predictive Density): Proper scoring rule measuring both accuracy and honest uncertainty; higher is better (each +100 is a clear improvement across thousands of apartments).
  • phase_map / 24-factor trace: Instead of estimating a separate noise multiplier for all 1,905 combinations of lease phase × year × listing flags, the model multiplies 24 independent factors and now traces only those 24 numbers during sampling.
  • open_scale: A single leak-free multiplier (e.g. 1.12×) applied to predictive uncertainty when scoring open asks, correcting for the empirically wider dispersion of open listings compared to closed training rows.
  • y1_pre2024: Allows historical calendar years (2014–2023, especially 2020–2022 Covid volatility) to have wider noise scales while keeping 2024–2026 fixed at the reference scale (1.0).
