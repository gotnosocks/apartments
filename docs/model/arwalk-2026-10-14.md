# Result: building level plus AR(1) deviation (2026-10-14)

I fit C with its half-year building walk replaced by a stationary AR(1) deviation around the
building level, once on Modal, and scored it against C. The design and the predictions, written
before the fit, are in `docs/model/predictions-arwalk-2026-10-14.md`.

## Convergence

The fit fails the gate:

- max split R-hat 1.041 on `walk_rho` (gate 1.01);
- min bulk ESS 39 on `walk_scale` (gate 400). `walk_rho` has ESS 44 and `building_scale` 69;
- max group R-hat 1.065 on a unit effect (gate 1.05). The walk and building groups stay under
  1.02.

The unit effect with the worst R-hat (1.144; the next worst is 1.03) belongs to 15 E 17th St #4.
Its row comes from a 2018 owner listing priced at $1,000 for 5 bedrooms. On the archived listing
page every event says $1,000, while the listing's own current price says $10,000, so the event
history most likely lost a digit. One chain held this unit's effect near -2.3 for its first 60
kept draws. A longer chain most likely would not fix a bad row, so this check waits on a data
fix. The trace of this row and five other low rows, with screens over the whole dataset, went to
the Data improvements thread, which decides the quarantine and correction rules.

The two chains agree:

| Parameter | Chain 1 median | Chain 2 median | Halves of each chain |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| rho | 0.8907 | 0.8904 | 0.891, 0.891, 0.891, 0.889 |
| sd | 0.0773 | 0.0772 | 0.077, 0.077, 0.078, 0.077 |
| building_scale | 0.1570 | 0.1573 | all 0.157 |

So the chains are mixing slowly, not settling in different places. Between kept draws, which are 9
sweeps apart, the autocorrelation is 0.87 for rho and 0.89 for sd. It falls to about 0.1 by lag 50.
Per sweep, the Metropolis steps on log(-log rho) given the deviation accept 15% of proposals. The
joint collapsed step over all scales accepts 17%, and the collapsed solo `walk_decay` update 48%.
Rho and sd move together, with a posterior correlation of +0.93, and log(-log rho) and log sd have
a correlation of -0.99. Each solo update moves one of them with the other held fixed, so it can
only take small steps across that ridge.

The estimates below come from a fit that fails the gate, so they are provisional. The chains agree
to about 0.002 on every quantity reported, which is small next to the differences from C.

## Parameters

Posterior medians with 80% intervals:

| Parameter | This fit | C |
|---|---|---|
| rho (per half-year) | 0.890 (0.884-0.898) | none |
| sd | 0.077 (0.076-0.079) | walk scale 0.031 (0.031-0.031) |
| building_scale | 0.157 (0.153-0.161) | 0.144 (0.140-0.148) |

corr(building_scale, sd) is -0.81.

A deviation decays with a half-life of about 3 years (log 0.5 / log 0.89 = 5.9 half-years). Its
standard deviation is 0.077 in log rent, about 8% of rent, and it does not grow with time. Next to
it, the level carries 0.157. The fit puts most of a building's persistent premium in the level and
uses the AR term for swings of a few years.

The long-lag check on C's residuals pointed to rho near 0.986. That check counted all of C's
building terms (level, walk, bedroom premium, slopes) together, so its slow decay mixed the level's
constant part with the walk. Fit as a level plus a deviation, the deviation decays much faster.

C's walk started at 0 in 2010 H1 and its variance grew with every half-year, so it widened a new
building's interval more and more for recent listings. The AR deviation has the same variance in
every half-year, which removes most of that rise. Recent rows are still over-covered at 90%
against a nominal 80, and the smallest buildings are still under-covered at 70-78%.

The variance shares move from "building over time" to "building":

- building rises from 10.2% to 12.8%;
- building over time falls from 5.6% to 3.2%.

Features (66.8% against 67.2%), market and time, and the neighbourhood labels barely change.
